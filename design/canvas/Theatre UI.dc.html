WHY THIS SHAPE
The marker never leaves the strip, so provenance is always on screen and always in the same place. The evidence is a keypress away and disappears again. Reading the strip is free; reading the evidence is a decision.
A cousin gets a diamond rather than a coloured dot because a diamond survives greyscale, colour blindness, a compressed screenshot, and a body drifting behind it. Faithful is dimmer than the abnormal states, so the normal case recedes and the two amber-equivalent states read as attention rather than failure: brighter ink, sharper fill, no hue, no red.
The popover and the warnings share one anchor on purpose. They are mutually exclusive: pressing P hides the warnings and shows the popover, which restates the same fact with its evidence. Two panels stacked in the same corner would have been two ways of saying one thing.

RESOLUTION · one stylesheet, chrome fixed in pixels
PanelSettings at Constant Pixel Size, scale 1. The type scale never changes at any resolution. One density step, written from C# as three custom-property overrides at panel init and on resize — not a second stylesheet: at panel width ≥ 2240, --strip-h 40→44, --panel-w 280→320, --pad-edge 16→20. 2240 rather than 2560 so an unmaximised 2560 window still gets it. Dropping the step entirely is acceptable — the 1920 chrome is correct at 2560, just tighter.
SHARED ANCHOR · the popover replaces the warnings
Both stay at --panel-top / --pad-edge and are mutually exclusive. P hides the warnings and shows the popover; Esc or P again reverses it. The popover restates everything a warning says and adds the recorded values, so stacking them would be two ways of saying one thing. Declaration order is six, not four: census, inspector, warnings, popover, strip, bar.
FOUR PROPERTIES USS LACKS · three resolved, one yours
line-height becomes a second Plex Sans FontAsset baked at 1.5 line spacing, used only by .prose. font-weight becomes the semibold mono asset on four elements: .ident__arm, .inspector__id, .plate__text, .census-row--hero .field__value; the other eleven uses were incidental and are gone. line-through becomes .struck + .struck__rule, a 1 px absolute child. text-align is notation for -unity-text-align. None of these needs an exception.
GLYPHS · confirmed, minus one
The set is deliberate. · carries the identity line and is load-bearing; − U+2212 is a true minus and only ever a depth; ≠ only ever a cousin badge. ✕ U+2715 is gone — the popover's mismatch mark is now × U+00D7, already in the set. Add U+2009 THIN SPACE: it is the thousands separator and appears in every grouped number.
