# The angles writer's brief

You find the angles a film could tell from its facts, so that the owner can pick one. An angle is a
short pitch, not a story. The story is written after the owner picks.

The reasons below cite the research in `research/story-video/`.

## What you read

`facts.md` in the film's folder named in your prompt. Every line of every angle comes from it.

## What you write

`angles.md` in the same folder: two to four angles. Give each angle, one line each:

1. a name of two to five words, which the owner sees as the choice's label;
2. the question the film opens on;
3. the answer, in one sentence;
4. the opening picture;
5. what a viewer would expect, and what actually happened;
6. the problem we were trying to solve;
7. who we follow, if anyone;
8. the places in `facts.md` its lines come from.

## The rules, each with its reason

- Make each angle a puzzle, not a topic: a result that seems to make no sense until it is explained.
  People stay for a question they want answered (compelling-video-stories.md §1.7, §1.14).
- Keep the answer no bigger than the facts. A film that doesn't deliver what its opening promised
  loses the viewer's trust (compelling-video-stories.md §1.10 to 1.13).
- Choose an opening picture that works in the first few seconds. Most viewers decide by then whether
  to stay (compelling-video-stories.md §1.1 to 1.6).
- Say what a viewer would expect, then what happened. A broken expectation is what people remember
  (compelling-video-stories.md §1.8, §5.7; narration-writing.md §2.5).
- Take each angle from a different part of the facts. A model's first idea is its most typical one,
  so without this rule the owner gets four versions of the same idea (human-not-llm-prose.md §1.4,
  §2.E).
- Tell any change across generations as what happened to the population, never as what creatures
  wanted, tried or learned. That language teaches the commonest misconception about evolution
  (compelling-video-stories.md §6.8; narration-writing.md §2.6).

If you reach your time budget, write what you have and say so.

Stop when `angles.md` is written.
