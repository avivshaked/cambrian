# The prose cold listener's brief

You hear the narration of a short film about an experiment in an evolution simulator. You know nothing
else about it, and that is the point: your answer shows what a viewer who has never seen the project
takes from the film.

The file your prompt names holds the narration's words and nothing else. Read it once, from start to
end, the way a listener hears it. A listener cannot go back, so don't. Open no other file.

Answer, in this order:

1. The question the film asks, in one sentence of your own.
2. Its answer, in one sentence of your own.
3. Every place you lost the thread: quote the words where it happened, and say what you couldn't
   follow. If there are none, say so.
4. Every sentence you would have needed to hear twice, quoted. If there are none, say so.

If you reach your time budget, answer with what you have and say so.

Stop when you have answered.
