# Proposal: when a world replaces the base, and when a change has to earn it

**2026-09-12, from the Astra review's policy section.** A ruling for DECISIONS; the agent's
recommendation is the first option.

## The inconsistency

D081 (2026-09-07) says a change joins the base world only when it matches the reference
world's passing-seed count. The practice since has been different. The grid became the base
after round 32 read two of five against the vertex world's four (D086, logbook/0079). Growth
became the base on round 33's five of five (D087). The box, the carrying current, the tank
and now the streams each replaced the base on the owner's ruling, and round 37 reads the
goal rule without requiring it (0093's M0). None of those was wrong. Each was a correction
to what the world *is*, or a change of scope, rather than a treatment competing on the
ecology's terms. The rule as written cannot say that, so a reader finds a rule and a
practice that disagree.

## The proposal

Two kinds of change, named in every pre-registration:

1. **A replacement** changes what the world is (its container, its fluid, its fields, its
   growth, a numerical repair). It becomes the base on the owner's ruling, its round is the
   new base round, and the goal rule is *read* in that round as a reading and not required.
   The round before it is its control for the mechanism predictions only. Examples: the
   grid, growth, the box, the tank, the streams with the fluid force, the transporter repair.
2. **A treatment** changes one price, one sense, one rule of the ecology on a fixed world.
   It is adopted into the base only when its round meets the bar its pre-registration
   names, which is D081's rule: the reference world's passing count, or a named mechanism
   bar when the goal rule is not the question. Examples: the link that earns, the stroke
   priced alone, a light sense, predation.

A round's entry says which kind it is in its first paragraph, and D081's clause is amended
to apply to treatments. Nothing in the record moves: the base rounds are what they are, and
the rule now says why.

## The alternatives

- **Keep D081 as written and call every replacement an exception.** Honest but noisy: six
  exceptions in six days is not a rule.
- **Drop the adoption bar altogether.** No: a treatment that does not earn its place would
  then join the base by being built, which is the drift D081 was written against.
