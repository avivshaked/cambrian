## What the owner has to decide (not decided here)

1. Higgs: the licence is research and non-commercial. The audition itself ("short-term evaluation and testing") is
   within it. Publishing a narrated film would need the Creator Use Grant's acknowledgement, and the grant excludes
   embedding the model in a distributed product. The reference-voice consent clause (IV(b)(ii)) applies to the
   20-second clip: the speaker's explicit consent must exist.
2. OmniVoice: CC-BY-NC weights, so a monetised channel is likely outside it; the audition alone is fine.
3. The three permissive models (Chatterbox MIT, VoxCPM2 and CosyVoice 3 Apache-2.0) carry no such question.
