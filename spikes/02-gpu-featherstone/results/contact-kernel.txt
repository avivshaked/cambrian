NOTES
 - 10,000 and 30,000 are made by tiling: the 3,945 bodies of the inscribed 118.35 m square are repeated, the
   N nearest the axis kept, in discs of radius 106.37 and 185.45 m. The bed there is the recorded tank's map
   read outside the tank. The density matches; the bed is not a real world's.
 - The serial mean (the CPU's summing order) runs on one thread and does not pay on the card. The tree mean
   moves only the cell's last bit, which can move candidate lists but never an overlap or a force.
 - Inference, not measured: the query is almost flat in N (284 to 342 us) and 30,000 bodies do not fill the
   card, so the time is the slowest thread's latency: the 64-cell bodies and their sort. The scan's single
   group is the next piece to grow with N.
