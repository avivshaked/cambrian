THE WHOLE BODY PHASE IN ONE KERNEL (fourth GPU spike, spike4/), 2026-09-23
===========================================================================

What was built
--------------
One ILGPU kernel, one thread a body, carrying DynamicsWorld.StepOne's body phase in the CPU's
order (src/Evosim.Dynamics/DynamicsWorld.cs:151-188), transcribed line for line:

  water pass     Water.Sample (no hold) -> CurrentField.VelocityAt / StreamsAt / StreamsSlopedUnit /
                 MapAt / StreamsUnit and AccelerationAt / StreamsAccelerationAt /
                 StreamsSlopedUnitWithGradient / StreamsUnitWithGradient / BedShape.HeightGradientAndHessian,
                 read at a pinned instant whose cosines, sines, envelopes and rates the host uploads
                 (186 numbers a step: the samplers' phase 2*pi*s/P and the acceleration's (2*pi/P)*s),
                 50 steps a block. Taken at the start of the body's own thread, which is the CPU's
                 separate pass because a body's water reads only that body.
  senses         CreatureSenses.Sample (the third spike's transcription)
  brain          Brain.Step (the third spike's transcription)
  drive          EffectorDrive.Drive: the ten-sample window and cursor, the limiter branch, the pair
                 torque, NoteDriveTorque
  fluid          Fluid.Apply: panels, NoteDrag, the drag limiter branch, D090's Morison term,
                 buoyancy and Restore
  contacts       Contacts.Apply with the instrument (overlap ids, bed/glass flag), ContactBed.Push,
                 ContactLaw; the push ADDED into Fext beside the drive and the fluid
  joint torques  DynamicsWorld.JointTorques with NotePassiveTorque
  solve          Aba.Seed / Inward / Outward (Outward parks q-dot-dot in tau, Settle reads it there),
                 Integrate
  kinematics     Kinematics.Poses, Kinematics.Velocities
  settle         Creature.Settle: the four accumulators in double in both builds
  trace          Creature.RecordTraceFrame for jointed bodies: a global ring, 3 frames x 16 links x 21
  finiteness     Creature.IsFinite, Alive = false and MarkLost
  sphere         Creature.RefreshContactSphere into the pending buffer

The grid build (memset, mean, ranges, one-group scan, scatter) is the third spike's; the commit is a
swap of two sphere buffers, and a dead body copies its committed sphere into the pending buffer so the
swap is the CPU's copy. The census (CloseContactStep) and the digest row stay serial on the host,
computed from the kernel's overlap lists and state.

Not carried: D100's water hold (round 45 runs at 0; the host refuses a hold), the rolls and the box's
transport field (the host refuses anything but the tank's streams with the vent off),
EffectorDrive.AppliedTorque (a probe readout nothing reads), a resize's flag on the trace frame
(nothing resizes inside a physics step; carried as 0).

Ceiling: 16 links and 48 degrees of freedom a body, 256 contact candidates a body (local), 64 overlap
ids a body (global, with a count that reports overflow); neuron stride = the crowd's largest brain
(75 at round 45 seed 2). Local memory, declared a thread (every local array at the ceiling, registers
and spills not counted): 28,572 bytes in double, 15,312 bytes in single.

Files: spike4/step.template (kernel and runner), spike4/gen4.py (writes StepD.cs, Gpu.Spike4.Dbl, and
StepF.cs, Gpu.Spike4.Sgl), spike4/StepHost.cs (flattening, the streams' instant, reflection reads),
spike4/StepSpike.cs (the driver). Program.cs dispatches "wholestep". spike3/BrainSpike.cs: MakeField
and ReadBirths made internal (no other change). No change to src/.

The crowd and the inputs
------------------------
runs\r45-s2\2026-09-22-232754-e51997d5 at 30,000 s: 6,145 bodies rebuilt from the snapshot and
poses.jsonl (Crowd3.Load), 280 jointed, links mean 3.09, max 16, dof max 12. The world: World(config,
seed 2), so the real current (Transport in a Tank, speed 0.1 m/s, the streams over the sloped bed) and
the real bed; the solver is SolverConfig.FromWorld(config, (double)0.01f, world.Bed, world) with the
contact instrument and its events on, as Simulation.cs:194-201 builds it. The world clock is set to
30,000 s and the step count to 3,000,000 through DynamicsWorld.ReadState.

Unrecorded inputs, declared: a reserve (9000 s x hash), a contact flag a part (hash < 0.1) and a damage
a part (hash < 0.05, then 1.2 x hash), all hashed from the id as the third spike hashes them; the
brain's clock is the body's age from lineage.jsonl; the snow field is the recorded column sums spread
evenly down each column. Velocities and joint rates are NOT synthetic: the bodies start at rest in
their recorded poses and move under the real current, their own drive and buoyancy (root displacement
over 1,000 steps: median 0.168 m, max 6.65 m).

    every value, count, flag, list and census: 513,659,879 values, 0 mismatches apart from the NaN pairs below
    the library's limiter counts at the end: drive 598, drag 13,405; 5 bodies lost, at the same step

    so the world's raw digest differs from the host's at all 300 steps (it hashes the lost bodies'

  The NaN difference is not a transcription difference: the source of every operation involved is
  the same text (Vec3 and V3 are the same one-line operators, Kinematics.Velocities and the kernel's
  Velocities the same order). BELIEVED, not proved: x64 propagates the first operand's NaN when both
  are NaN, and the JIT is free to swap the operands of an addition, so which NaN survives depends on
  the compiled code, not the source. Consequence for the port: a raw-bit digest is not an identity
  check across two compilations once a body has gone non-finite; hash lost bodies' NaNs canonically,
  or drop lost bodies from the digest.
