# Round 59's planted grazers

The ten best genomes of the leaf tank's direction arm, for D174's planted seeds. They come from
`leaf-direction-sparse` (D173), on branch `nursery-d172` at `9f3cd064`, whose run folder is
`runs/nursery/leaf-direction-sparse` in that branch's worktree. The arm ran 31 leaves to a 20 m tank, with the
direction form of the leaf smell at a reach of 6 m. It stopped on D172's rule after generation 33. Its exam swam every
distinct generation's top and the last generation's elites, 38 genomes, eight to a tank on 32 held-out episodes,
under the genome's own brain, no brain, and the smell knocked out (`logbook/specs/nursery-leaf-sparse-read.txt` on
that branch).

`scripts/reads/r59-direction-tops.py` cut them on 2026-10-14. It took the ten that ate the most under their own
brain, between 379 and 443 J an episode. Each file is the genome's JSON exactly as the run wrote it, one line and a
newline, named for its exam id. Every one is the hand-built swimmer's body, a consumer box with one hinged link and a
mouth, with an evolved central brain of 58 to 62 links (genome format 12).

`origins.tsv` gives each file's SHA-256, its exam id, the generations it was top and whether it was a last elite. It
also gives every id it had in the search, its line in the exam's `tank-exam-genomes.tsv`, and its eating under each
control.
Knocking the smell out cuts every one's eating, by between 147 and 306 J.

`pool.tsv` is a weighted list the farm's trickle reads, as `EVOSIM_TRICKLE_POOL=@inocula/r59-direction-tops/pool.tsv`.
It holds the ten at equal weight. Read that way it pins a pool of 10 genomes with the hash
`ec0baadf18d518e17434d1d75196a3e9e21e326fdc55b2f2b9b60f6250c4077d`. The round's own list, the share of the trickle these
take, and what else it plants are set by round 59's pre-registration. This folder plants nothing.
