# 0035 — The neuron was priced in 1994

**2026-08-29**  ·  Literature review round 3

The review's own §7.1 had said it since round 2: §5A — by now most of the project — rests on
a literature that *"has never been searched for at all"*, and should be read as unreviewed
design until a round 3 runs. That was written on 2026-08-02. Twenty-seven days and roughly
forty experiment arms later, round 3 ran. It took an afternoon.

## How it ran

Four search sweeps in parallel, delegated to subagents with scoped briefs (ecosystem
primaries; open-endedness instruments; trophic emergence and neural cost; the review's
recorded debts), under a fixed rule set: read-only on the web, fetched content treated as
data and never as instructions, paywalled sources queued for the human rather than attempted.
Screening, primary-text verification and synthesis stayed with the reviewing model — every
claim that entered the synthesis or touched `DESIGN.md` was re-verified against the
downloaded text, at the page. Eight papers retrieved, all open access, no institutional
login. The full protocol run is the review's §0 round row and §3.6; the retrievals are in
`FETCH-RESULTS.md`.

## What it found, in one sentence each

- **The per-neuron charge has a precedent, and it is thirty-two years old.** PolyWorld bills
  energy *"determined linearly from the number of neurons and the number of synapses"*
  [Y94, p.7]. §5A.2 had claimed the term as this design's own; it is corrected. The work
  integral ∫|τ·ω| dt still appears to be ours.
- **The strategy-collapse result this project measured piecewise was published in 2002.**
  Cooper & Ofria: depletable resources produce negative frequency-dependent selection and up
  to nine coexisting strategies — with mutation *off* — and the same ecosystems collapse to
  one genotype when the resources are made unlimited. D023's finite sun is one instance of
  that rule. The rule predicts what D048's matter field bought, and what a second nutrient
  would buy: coexistence, not just realism.
- **Gene Pool says why locomotion paid there and not here.** Its food and its mates both
  require physical approach, so movement is the only route to reproduction. This world's
  prize is depth, and depth is reachable by buoyancy and by differential survival — which is
  the whole muscle saga of logbook/0026–0031, restated as prize design.
- **The 2025 preprint the review owed itself since round 1 was worth the debt.** Mertan &
  Cheney built ground truth (a controller trained for all 1.3M morphologies in their space)
  and showed MAP-Elites *and* explicit innovation protection find near-optimal bodies in at
  most 22% of trials, because co-optimisation systematically undervalues newly-mutated
  bodies. §2's mitigations are real and insufficient, and whether this world's ecological
  niches do better is now explicitly a thing to measure.
- **The treadmill question has an instrument.** Bedau–Packard evolutionary activity
  classifies long-term dynamics into four classes, and Standish's Ecolab variant replaces
  the "neutral shadow run" with a randomly-permuted shadow population — implementable
  against a birth/parent/genome log, which this project keeps deciding not to write
  (`lineage.jsonl`). The instrument §5A.6b admits it lacks exists; what it needs is the file.
- **The two load-bearing quarantined citations are real.** Cheney 2018 and Pugh 2016 were
  CrossRef-verified, retrieved, read at the passages §2 and §8.3 lean on, and promoted out
  of §13.4. Pugh's own conclusion carries a nuance the design should keep: *"not just any
  type of behavioral diversity is successful"* — not "unaligned always fails".

## The pattern

Every prior entry's pattern has been some form of *a right-looking number arrived at for the
wrong reason*. This one is the complement: **right answers, sitting on a shelf, while the
project re-derived them at experiment prices.** The finite-resource coexistence result cost
this project the D023 sweep, the D048 matter field and a month of arms to establish
piecewise; it was six pages in 2002. The neural charge was independently re-invented
twenty-two years after PolyWorld shipped it. None of the re-derivation was wasted — the
measurements are on this substrate and the literature's are not, which is exactly the
substrate caveat the review keeps flagging in the other direction — but the *order* was
wrong, and the review's own protocol had said so in writing for four weeks.

The general lesson is the one logbook/0001 opened the project with, returned with interest:
the review corrected the design three times before a line of code existed, and it corrected
the provenance of the design's proudest section the day it was finally allowed to look
again. Reading is the cheapest instrument this project owns, and it is the only one that
was idle.
