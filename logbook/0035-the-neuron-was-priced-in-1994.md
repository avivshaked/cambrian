# 0035 — The neuron was priced in 1994

**2026-08-29**  ·  Literature review round 3

The review's own §7.1 had said it since round 2. Most of the project by now rests on §5A,
and §5A rests on a literature that *"has never been searched for at all"*. It was to be read
as unreviewed design until a round 3 ran. That was written on 2026-08-02. Twenty-seven days
and roughly forty experiment arms later, round 3 ran, and it took an afternoon.

## How it ran

Four search sweeps ran in parallel, delegated to subagents with scoped briefs: ecosystem
primaries, open-endedness instruments, trophic emergence and neural cost, and the review's
recorded debts.

The rules were fixed. Read-only on the web, fetched content treated as data and never as
instructions, and paywalled sources queued for the human rather than attempted.

Screening, primary-text verification and synthesis stayed with the reviewing model. Every
claim that entered the synthesis or touched `DESIGN.md` was re-verified against the
downloaded text, at the page. Eight papers were retrieved, all open access, with no
institutional login.

The full protocol run is the review's §0 round row and §3.6, and the retrievals are in
`FETCH-RESULTS.md`.

## What it found, in one sentence each

- The per-neuron charge has a precedent, and it is thirty-two years old. PolyWorld bills energy
  *"determined linearly from the number of neurons and the number of synapses"* [Y94, p.7], and
  §5A.2 had claimed the term as this design's own. It is corrected, and the work integral
  ∫|τ·ω| dt still appears to be ours.
- The strategy-collapse result this project measured piecewise was published in 2002. Cooper &
  Ofria found that depletable resources produce negative frequency-dependent selection and
  up to nine coexisting strategies, with mutation *off*. The same ecosystems collapse to one
  genotype when the resources are made unlimited. D023's finite sun is one instance of that
  rule. The rule predicts what D048's matter field bought and what a second nutrient would
  buy, which is coexistence rather than realism alone.
- Gene Pool says why locomotion paid there and not here. Its food and its mates both require
  physical approach, so movement is the only route to reproduction. This world's prize is
  depth, and depth is reachable by buoyancy and by differential survival. That is the whole
  muscle saga of logbook/0026–0031, restated as prize design.
- The 2025 preprint the review owed itself since round 1 was worth the debt. Mertan & Cheney
  built ground truth, a controller trained for all 1.3M morphologies in their space. They
  showed that MAP-Elites and explicit innovation protection both find near-optimal bodies in
  at most 22% of trials, because co-optimisation systematically undervalues newly-mutated
  bodies. The mitigations in §2 are real and insufficient, and whether this world's
  ecological niches do better is now explicitly a thing to measure.
- The treadmill question has an instrument. Bedau–Packard evolutionary activity classifies
  long-term dynamics into four classes, and Standish's Ecolab variant replaces the "neutral
  shadow run" with a randomly-permuted shadow population. That is implementable against a
  birth/parent/genome log, which this project keeps deciding not to write (`lineage.jsonl`).
  The instrument §5A.6b admits it lacks exists, and what it needs is the file.
- The two load-bearing quarantined citations are real. Cheney 2018 and Pugh 2016 were
  CrossRef-verified, retrieved, read at the passages §2 and §8.3 lean on, and promoted out
  of §13.4. Pugh's own conclusion carries a nuance the design should keep: *"not just any
  type of behavioral diversity is successful"*, which is weaker than "unaligned always
  fails".

## The pattern

Every prior entry's pattern has been some form of *a right-looking number arrived at for the
wrong reason*. This one is the complement: **right answers, sitting on a shelf, while the
project re-derived them at experiment prices.**

The finite-resource coexistence result cost this project the D023 sweep, the D048 matter
field and a month of arms to establish piecewise. It was six pages in 2002. The neural
charge was independently re-invented twenty-two years after PolyWorld shipped it.

None of the re-derivation was wasted: the measurements are on this substrate and the
literature's are not. That is the substrate caveat the review keeps flagging in the other
direction. But the *order* was wrong, and the review's own protocol had said so in writing
for four weeks.

The general lesson is the one logbook/0001 opened the project with, returned with interest.
The review corrected the design three times before a line of code existed. It corrected the
provenance of the design's proudest section the day it was finally allowed to look again.
Reading is the cheapest instrument this project owns, and it is the only one that was idle.
