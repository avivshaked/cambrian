# Specs and build reports

The briefs builds were made from, and the reports those builds handed back. A `*-spec.md` is
what an implementing agent was given before it wrote a line of code; a `*-build-report.md` is
what it returned. Surveys, pre-registration drafts, review responses and the per-round
results notes sit here for the same reason: entries cite them, and a citation nobody can
follow is worth nothing.

None of it is a source of truth. [`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) says what the system is and
[`DECISIONS.md`](../../DECISIONS.md) says why it is that. A spec says what somebody was asked
to build on one morning, which is often not what got built; a build report says what came
back, before anyone had read it against a world. Where either disagrees with the design
documents, the design documents win.

Nothing here is edited after the fact, for the reason logbook entries are not: a brief
rewritten to match what happened stops being evidence that the asking came first.
Paths inside these files were rewritten once, on 2026-09-10, when the files themselves moved
out of `scratch/`; nothing else was touched.
