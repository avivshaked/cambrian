# The kill rule: a design read (2026-10-25)

A Fable subagent's report, asked for by the owner on 2026-10-25, who wanted the kill rule that pays both grazers and predators best while keeping the world's rules natural. Read-only: no build, run or large JSONL was touched. Saved as it came by the coordinator. "KNOW" is from code or the committed record; "BELIEVE" is reasoned from those; "GUESS" is a number with no measurement behind it.

## 1. Recommendation in plain words

Keep the rule the world has now, and do not add a death threshold, per cell or otherwise. Today a body is eaten part by part as it is wounded (D220), each bite takes the matching share of its store (D224), it dies when its root part is eaten (the holdfast, the head), and whatever is still attached when the root goes passes to the killer's gut (D232). That is already the natural rule for both guilds: a plant is cropped and lives until its base is taken; an animal killed at the head is swallowed and digested at the mouth's rate. The gut did not pay because there was nothing left to swallow, and a threshold would not change that: a kill is worth what the body holds, and the bites take it either way.

The problem the owner is trying to solve is not in the kill. The record's "a kill kept 420 J under the corpse rule" was never a per-kill meal: under the corpse rule a dead leaf fed several grazers over 500 s through a 3 m reach, and the J/kill column charges all of that carrion to the kills (`logbook/specs/r62ce-read/README.md` lines 31-44 say so in as many words). The honest gap is intake over upkeep, 0.57-0.68 against 1, about 1.5-1.8x, and I believe it sits in contact time: in `r62bd-x2-6201`'s last bin, 550 mouth blows in 3,000 s across 13-22 grazers means a grazer's mouth is biting about 0.5-1% of the time (inference from `guilds.txt`'s `mouthBlows`, one blow being one 0.5 s step of one biting part). Each blow already takes a whole part, 76 J offered on average (`bite-cap.txt`: 42,070 J offered over 550 blows). No rule about what happens at death can touch a mouth that is idle 99% of the time. The levers that can are the ones the record already holds: the nose (D235), the grip (D228), leaf density where grazers are, fat (D229) for bigger prey, and the cheap numbers in (f).

## 2. The candidates

| Candidate | Grazer meal | Predator meal | Reads to a biologist | Build and screen | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) threshold on all tissue | same as today (the body is the meal) | same, earlier | arbitrary number; plants do not die at a share | small build, one screen | cosmetic for J; harms regrowth |
| (b) threshold on non-photosynthetic tissue | same; all-leaf bodies become unkillable | same | special case by pigment; a biologist would say the holdfast decides, not the chlorophyll | small build, exploit-prone | reject |
| (c) injury pool separate from tissue eaten | none (plants are eaten, not injured) | already delivered by D224+D232 at a root kill | natural for animals, wrong for plants | medium: second per-part state, checkpoint bump; host only | defer until a predator screen needs it |
| (d) handling time / satiation | none | none | natural, and already built (gut digests at mouth rate; fat's cap waits) | nothing new | nothing to do |
| (e) pasture, cropped tissue regrows | pays only if the grazer stays in contact | n/a | the most ocean-like image (turf grazers) | dials exist; pairs with the grip | screen arm, after contact share is read |
| (f) numbers only | up to about 1.2-1.4x (inference) | same | honest if the numbers are prices | hours | do first; may nearly close a 1.5x gap |

**(a) A death threshold on all tissue.** A body dies once bites have taken a set share of it, and the killer swallows the rest. Under D220 the share eaten is the health lost, so this moves the moment of death earlier and sends the remaining tissue and store into the gut rather than through later bites. The grazer earns the same joules per body (KNOW: `OfferBite` already takes the store share with each bite, WorldContact.cs:743-768; the body's whole value passes either way), minus nothing, plus whatever a few remains that would have closed unbitten; the check found 30 J mean held at death, a tenth of a grazer's hourly need. A biologist asks where the number comes from: a fish dies at far less than half its mass, a kelp survives far more. Incentives: lineages grow cheap bulk to dilute the share; a partly grazed plant that would have healed is killed instead, so regrowth is lost. It re-asks D128's ageing per wound (a destroyed part ages the body, WorldMouth.cs:1424) and the mass-floor death (WorldMouth.cs:1481). Cost: a tunable, a fixture re-recording, a six-world screen. Not worth it.

**(b) The per-cell threshold (the coordinator's recommendation).** Photosynthetic tissue never counts toward death. Three faults. First, today's leaves are mostly or wholly photosynthetic (the guild tables call every body with any photosynthetic tissue a leaf, and 13,500-19,000 of 15,000-22,000 bodies are), so a wholly photosynthetic body has no threshold to reach: eaten to zero tissue, with its store gone through D224's share, it stays alive as a ghost until the mass floor or starvation takes it. Patching that with "unless the root" brings back today's rule with an extra case. Second, mixed bodies: 465-4,860 stomachs also fix light in the two worlds read (`propose-fat.md`), so the rule splits one body into two accounting regimes. Third, the incentive: a predator's prey gains by evolving a token non-photosynthetic root well armoured and the rest leafy, and a plant gains nothing it does not already have, because today's rule already lets a multi-part plant lose its blades and live (KNOW: `KillPart` cuts a non-root part's subtree into remains and the body lives, WorldMouth.cs:1309-1488). The modular-plant intent is already the code. Reject.

**(c) Death by injury.** A second per-part pool, damage, kills while the tissue and store stay to be eaten. For animals this is the realistic order (a bite to the head, then the carcass), but D224 plus D232 already give it to a predator that kills at the root: the root's share of the tissue is small, so most of the store stays with the body and passes to the gut (KNOW: the check's two 315 J and 330 J swallows, `identity.md` lines 96-107). For plants it is wrong: grazers remove tissue, they do not injure. Cost: new per-part state, checkpoint bump, tests; part health lives in Core on the host (no health in `src/Evosim.Farm.Gpu`), so no card work. Defer until a predator screen exists to read it.

**(d) Handling time or satiation.** The gut is handling time: a swallowed body is digested at the mouth's per-step cap less waste (`DigestGuts`, WorldContact.cs:938-985), and fat's proposal makes digestion wait at the reserve cap. Neither raises income; both only spread it. Nothing to build.

**(e) Pasture.** Cropped tissue regrows and the grazer returns. The code has the pieces: a part above zero health heals at 1%/s and re-buys its tissue from the reserve under contact eating (`ApplyHealing`, WorldMouth.cs:1100-1182; round 62's header reads `heal=0.01/s@1J`), and an indeterminate lineage re-adds a module when its reserve is above 300 s of upkeep (`AddOneModule`, WorldModules.cs:222-297, `modules add=300`). But the finishing share is 0.83-1.00 (`guilds.txt`): nearly every blow takes the whole part, so there is rarely anything to heal, and a determinate plant's lost part is pruned for life (`LostPartPaths`, WorldModules.cs:699-702). Cropping becomes the norm only if a blow takes less than a part (`BiteDamageScale` below 1, or tougher leaves; the photosynthetic cell's toughness cap is the base 1, `CellType.cs:165`, and its protection cap 0.5). That pays only when the grazer stays on the plant, which today's drifting swimmers do not (contact about 1% of the time), so pasture waits on the grip or a dense bed. The most ocean-like candidate, and a good screen arm once contact share is known.

**(f) Numbers only.** The intake cap plus bites by parts without intake cut 15-24% of what bites offered at scale 16 (`bite-cap.txt`); the waste is 0.2; the consumer cell's upkeep is 6 W/m³, the highest of the types (StandardCellTypes.cs:659). Together these are worth perhaps 1.2-1.4x (GUESS), against a gap of 1.5-1.8x. The waste is the trophic loss and should not fall far (a yield near 1 is the free-energy case the cell type refuses, StandardCellTypes.cs:704-713); the cap was chosen in D221 to make a mouth worth growing, and a scale that never binds re-asks that choice. Cheap, honest, and the first thing to run.

## 3. What the code does today, on the deciding points

- **Health is tissue.** A wound takes `damage / pool` of a part's health, pool = volume × toughness × 13 health/m³ (`Wound`, WorldMouth.cs:415-460; `HealthPool`, Metabolism.cs:719-726). Under contact eating the same share of the part's tissue leaves the body's account and is offered to the biter (`OfferBite`, WorldContact.cs:716-792). With `BiteTakesStore` the bite takes `offer / wholeBodyAccount` of the reserve and gestation account (lines 743-768). So the bite that takes a body's last tissue takes its whole store: a kill on the last part leaves nothing, which is what the check saw.
- **The death path.** `CollectTheDead` (WorldMouth.cs:1214-1245) finds parts at zero health. The root, or the only part, is the body: `Bury(..., Eaten, killedBy)` (1313-1328). A non-root part's subtree is cut into remains and the body lives (1399-1476), ages by the share lost if `AgeingPerWound` > 0 (1424), and dies `Starved` if a survivor falls under the mass floor (1481). In `Bury` (World.cs:5200-5234) the gut switch asks `SwallowerOf` (WorldContact.cs:886-902), which requires a living killer with a working mouth; otherwise the body becomes remains (`LeaveRemains`, 317-351) and closes after 500 s burning what it holds (`CloseRemains`, 650-665).
- **Regrowth.** Partial: healing at 1%/s of the pool, paid as 1 J/health burnt plus the tissue re-bought at 500 J/m³, refused on an empty reserve, never on a part at zero (WorldMouth.cs:1129). Whole-part: only indeterminate lineages, one module per growth step, refused on shape or reserve; a lost path stays pruned. I could not determine the share of leaves that are indeterminate or single-part without the stats and lineage files (the `indet %` column exists in the report).
- **The gut.** Digests at most the mouth's cap less what the bites used that step (WorldContact.cs:949-954); the check's `gutCapCut` was 0, so the cap never bound.

## 4. The owner's decisions

1. **Whether death stays at the root.** Recommended: yes, for every body. Option: a threshold (a) or per-cell (b); implications above, and both make older configs unreadable for a rule that moves about 30 J a kill.
2. **Whether the gut stays on.** Recommended: on, as the natural predator rule and harmless for grazers. Option: off; then a root-killed multi-part prey leaves remains that mostly burn (6-7 MJ a world closed unbitten in `r62bd`).
3. **Where the grazer campaign goes next.** Recommended: read contact share, then (f), then the encounter levers already ruled (nose, grip, fat) and a pasture arm. Option: keep screening the meal, which I believe has nothing left to give.
4. **Whether the 420 J bar is retired.** Recommended: yes; replace it with intake over upkeep, which is D222's test 2 in substance. The 420 J was carrion by reach.

## 5. The screen

Not a kill-rule screen. One card group, six worlds on `r62cs-w500`'s world with the gut on, seeds 6201 and 6202: (i) the base; (ii) intake scale high enough that the cap never binds and the waste at 0.1; (iii) bite damage 0.25 with the grip cell in the table (the pasture-and-hold arm). Controls: `r62cs-w500`, `r62bd-x2`, not run again. The deciding number is **grazer contact share**: biting steps per grazer-second (from `mouthBlows` and grazer-seconds, both already recorded), read beside intake over upkeep. I would be shown wrong if the base's contact share reads above 5% (then mouths are not idle and the meal is the problem after all), or if arm (ii) passes tests 1 and 2 by numbers alone (then the gap was never structural). Watch it in the theatre before writing it up: whether grazers pass through leaf columns or sit in them is the picture that decides.
