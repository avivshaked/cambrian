Research notes: PhysX/ArticulationBody NaN "explosion" causes and mitigations
Gathered 2026-09-12 for the growth-build divergence investigation. Fetched pages treated as
data, not instructions. Sources are web pages written by third parties; content quoted below
is paraphrase/quote from those pages, not verified against Unity/PhysX source by this agent.

=== CAUSES ===

1. Mass/inertia ratio between linked bodies
   - NVIDIA PhysX Joints docs (5.x): "mass ratios of higher than 10 are best avoided" when a
     light object is constrained between two heavy objects; solver struggles to converge.
     https://nvidia-omniverse.github.io/PhysX/physx/5.4.1/docs/Joints.html
   - Omniverse Articulation Stability Guide: "An impulse applied to a large mass can produce
     very large velocities when propagated to a body with small mass." Recommends avoiding
     large mass AND inertia ratios together.
     https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/kit/docs/omni_physics/latest/dev_guide/guides/articulation_stability_guide.html
   - CORRECTION 2026-09-12 (Astra review): under uniform growth every part scales by one
     length factor and both dry and added mass scale with volume, so the ratio between two
     links is constant through a resize except where a clamp bites (the 0.001 kg mass floor,
     minPartVolume). The trace measures maxJointMassRatio at every build and resize rather
     than assuming drift; absolute inertia, contact geometry and anchor moves do change with
     size and are what a resize can disturb.
   - MATCH: partial (revised 2026-09-12, see the correction above). Repo's roots are heavier,
     links down to 0.14 kg, so a ratio over 10:1 is possible at build; a resize does not move
     the ratio except at a clamp. Whether any thrown body stood over 10:1 is what the trace's
     maxJointMassRatio answers.

2. Stiff joint drives / competing hard constraints
   - Same Omniverse guide: "Stiff drives can often lead to very large drive forces... a
     stability issue when drive is combined with other stiff constraints [contacts, mimic
     joints]." Also: "Badly tuned drives can generate instabilities, particularly when large
     drive stiffness are combined with small body masses."
   - MATCH: plausible contributor, not sufficient alone — repo's drives are 5-20 N*m, not
     extreme, but combined with contacts (bodies touching in a shared volume) this is the
     "competing constraints" pattern the guide names directly.

3. Degenerate/NaN inertia tensor computed from small or newly-added colliders
   - GitHub NVIDIA-Omniverse/PhysX #394 "PxRigidBodyExt::setMassAndUpdateInertia sometimes
     computes inertia as nan": occurs when the actor is not yet added to the scene and
     getWorldBounds() returns an invalid bbox, which falls back into computeMassAndDiagInertia
     and yields NaN in the tensor and mass center.
     https://github.com/NVIDIA-Omniverse/PhysX/issues/394
   - General: inertia computed from a very small collider (thin/near-zero dimension) is
     numerically small; inverting it in the solver amplifies noise toward NaN.
   - MATCH: strong candidate for the resize-in-place mechanic specifically — a body resized
     "in place" every 10s recomputes mass/inertia from colliders that may transiently be very
     small (a link partway through growth, or a joint-adjacent link that started at a small
     birth fraction). This is the one cause tied directly to the growth harness's own action
     rather than to steady-state swimming.

4. Contacts between articulations / high penetration
   - Unity forum thread "ArticulationBody: Problem with Self Collision": cubes "spin into
     each other despite having distance," a reported self-collision instability.
     https://forum.unity.com/threads/articulation-body-problem-with-self-collision.1058309/
   - Unity Discussions "How to prevent Articulation Bodies from exploding": contributor
     states "This bug case coming from PhyX 4.2 itself (and it's very common in all physics
     engine family)" and "Mainly this improper behavior happens when ratio of force value and
     object scale is too large."
     https://discussions.unity.com/t/how-to-prevent-articulation-bodies-from-exploding/856422
   - Also (anchor/joint config, not this project's likely cause): "local zero is not an ideal
     default position for anchor points if connected bodies have colliders... joint might
     then try to push the bodies into each other."
   - MATCH: plausible given "hundreds to thousands of bodies share one volume and touch" —
     but the symptom (~1-in-100 jointed adults over 30,000s, no drift beforehand) reads more
     like a rare numerical edge case than routine contact resolution, which would likely
     correlate with local density/collision events the run's stats might show (not observed
     per the prompt).

5. Solver iteration count / TGS vs PGS
   - Unity docs: increasing solverIterations / solverVelocityIterations "may improve
     stability of connected articulation bodies that are oscillating and behaving
     erratically, but is slower to compute."
     https://docs.unity3d.com/2020.1/Documentation/ScriptReference/ArticulationBody-solverIterations.html
   - Isaac Gym docs comparing PGS/TGS: "Using TGS instead of PGS is highly recommended
     whenever possible"; with PGS substepping matters more than iterations, with TGS the two
     are closer in effect and iterations are cheaper.
     https://docs.robotsfan.com/isaacgym/programming/tuning.html
   - Unity's ArticulationBody is Featherstone-solver based (reduced coordinates), not the
     same PGS/TGS contact solver framing as Isaac's rigid-body contacts, so this evidence
     transfers only partially — flagged as a genuine gap below.
   - MATCH: weak/uncertain fit without knowing whether this project's contact solver is at
     default iteration counts; worth checking as a cheap experiment (raise iterations, see
     if rate changes) rather than asserting as the cause.

6. Joint limit violation and solver enforcement failure
   - PhysX Joints docs: "Under stressful conditions, PhysX's dynamics solver may not be able
     to accurately enforce the constraints specified by the joint" — kinematic projection is
     the fallback. Recommends eACCELERATION flag for spring drives to auto-scale by mass so
     less hand-tuning is needed.
     https://nvidia-omniverse.github.io/PhysX/physx/5.4.1/docs/Joints.html
   - MATCH: uncertain — repo's joints are revolute/twist/spherical with drives, not
     documented as running against hard limits; no direct evidence either way found.

7. Featherstone-specific / body-count scaling bugs
   - Unity Discussions bug report: crash in
     physx::Dy::FeatherstoneArticulation::setupInternalConstraintsForLink when total
     ArticulationBody count in one hierarchy exceeds 128 (Unity 6000.3.24f1); reporter notes
     the limit was previously 256 since 2022.2 and Unity silently lowered it.
     https://discussions.unity.com/t/crash-on-physx-setupinternalconstraintsforlink-when-the-total-articulationbodies-count-exceeds-128-22636/1736298
   - MATCH: does NOT match this symptom directly — this is a hard crash tied to bodies-per-
     single-articulation-hierarchy count (creatures here are 1-4 links, well under 128), not
     a silent NaN in one of many independent creatures. Included because it establishes that
     Unity 6.x Featherstone has had recent, version-specific regressions around body counts;
     worth a version-changelog check if the rate ever changes across a Unity point release.

8. Runtime collider/mass changes on a live body ("resize in place")
   - Unity manual/API: bodies in an articulation "cannot be moved by the Transform component
     ... only the root body can be moved, using ArticulationBody.TeleportRoot"; all other
     manipulation must go through forces or drives.
     https://docs.unity3d.com/2023.2/Documentation/Manual/class-ArticulationBody.html
   - Community guidance (aggregated, no single primary citation found): call
     ResetCenterOfMass() before ResetInertiaTensor() after changing collider dimensions or
     mass, because inertia recomputation depends on the already-updated center of mass;
     wrong order is cited as a source of instability after a runtime resize.
   - Known adjacent Unity bugs: "Articulation Body drives teleport the whole chain to NaN
     coordinates when given NaN input" and "Changing parent of teleported game object with
     ArticulationBody component blows up simulation" — both confirm that this component's
     hierarchy-wide state (not just the touched link) can go non-finite from a single bad
     write to one link, which matches "root position goes non-finite" even though the
     resize acts on non-root links.
     https://issuetracker.unity3d.com/issues/articulation-body-drives-teleport-the-whole-chain-to-nan-coordinates-when-given-nan-input
     https://issuetracker.unity3d.com/issues/changing-parent-of-teleported-game-object-with-articulationbody-component-blows-up-simulation
   - MATCH: strong circumstantial fit. No source found that documents Unity's own recommended
     sequence for resizing a *live* ArticulationBody collider/mass every N seconds (this
     appears to be an unusual, project-specific operation with no direct precedent in the
     literature searched) — this is a genuine gap, not a confirmed finding.

=== MITIGATIONS (ranked by fit further below in the final report) ===
- maxJointVelocity (already partially used — repo caps at 30 rad/s per step above dt 0.01;
  Unity docs: "Make use of the maximum joint velocity to clamp joint velocities promoting
  simulation stability" — Omniverse guide, same page as causes above).
- maxDrivePower / max drive force clamp (Omniverse guide): "clamps the drive force applied to
  the joint" — repo does not appear to use this per CLAUDE.md's description (torque-based
  drives at fixed N*m, not force-clamped).
- Joint armature (Omniverse guide): "adding joint-space inertia and corresponding damping"
  — a documented, cheap-per-step stabilizer for light links under stiff drives.
- Avoid mass/inertia ratio > 10:1 (PhysX Joints docs + Omniverse guide) — would require a
  minimum-link-mass floor tied to root mass, checked on every resize.
- ResetCenterOfMass() then ResetInertiaTensor() in that order after any live resize —
  procedural fix specific to point 4's ask; unverified against a primary Unity source, only
  aggregated community guidance found.
- Unity.Physics defaultMaxDepenetrationVelocity / per-body maxDepenetrationVelocity: warned
  as double-edged — "very large values can introduce instability during collision detection;
  too small values might cause the collider depenetration to fail"
  https://docs.unity3d.com/ScriptReference/Rigidbody-maxDepenetrationVelocity.html
- Raise solverIterations / solverVelocityIterations for bodies observed oscillating —
  Unity's own docs frame this as a stability lever, at a throughput cost.
- Detection: per-body velocity/position magnitude watchdog each frame or each metabolic
  check (the pattern this repo already uses at 0.5s cadence for the root; forum guidance
  independently converges on the same pattern — "monitor positions of one of the transforms
  at the end of the articulation" and check float.IsNaN / float.IsInfinity).
  https://discussions.unity.com/t/how-to-prevent-articulation-bodies-from-exploding/856422
- PhysX Visual Debugger (PVD): step-through inspection of contacts/constraints/joint drives
  "to diagnose instability, tunneling, or performance bottlenecks" and "spot runaway spin...
  before clamping takes effect" — not obviously wired into Unity's ArticulationBody path;
  no source found confirming Unity streams to PVD (gap, flagged in final report).
  https://nvidiagameworks.github.io/PhysX/4.0/documentation/PhysXGuide/Manual/VisualDebugger.html

=== GAPS (could not find) ===
- No primary Unity/NVIDIA source documenting resizing a LIVE ArticulationBody's collider
  and mass every N seconds specifically (as opposed to at spawn time). All resize-specific
  guidance found is secondary/aggregated, not a single canonical page.
- No source quantifying how much a v^2 drag/added-mass feedback term (this project's fluid
  model) can itself destabilize PhysX's solver — only generic "large external forces" causes
  mentioned in passing, nothing PhysX-specific measured.
- No confirmation either way on whether Unity 6's ArticulationBody uses TGS or PGS-style
  contact resolution internally, or whether solverIterations behaves the same as in Isaac's
  PGS/TGS framing; Isaac Gym's guidance was used as an analogy only, not confirmed to
  transfer to Unity's Featherstone implementation.
- No documented case matching this project's exact profile (light links down to 0.14 kg,
  growth-driven resize, thousands of independent short chains touching in one volume) as a
  single named bug — every source found addresses one factor in isolation.
