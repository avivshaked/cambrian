Why a resume over overflows parts from its run: Scatter (gpu-kernel.template ~962) fills each hash bucket by atomic
insert, so the order a query meets its candidates in is a race; under the cap the query sorts them, past it the first
256 to arrive were kept. A query also met a row once for every cell the two share, and each arrival took a slot
before the old sort and dedup, so duplicates filled the cap.
