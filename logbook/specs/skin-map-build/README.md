# Genome to skin: the build so far

The theatre's skin plan, section 5 (`logbook/specs/theatre-skin-plan.md`), designs a skin drawn from the genome. The aim is
that relatives look alike and strangers look different. The coordinator moved it up on 2026-10-29, at the owner's request,
and the theatre lane (evolution-simulator-c8) built it the same day. This note says what exists, what was measured and what
is still open.

## What exists

- **The Python read** is `scripts/skin-map/skin_features.py`, with its synthetic test `test_skin_features.py` beside it. It reads a run's
  genomes and grows each adult body with a port of Core's development. It takes 26 features: cell shares, body-plan measures,
  investment and margin, and the brain's size and weight summaries. It fits the projection, makes the ten-number skin vector
  and computes the check of 5(e).
  - Each brain-weight z shrinks toward the population by n/(n+4), where n is the brain's connection count. Without the
    shrink, a brain of one to three links swung rim hue and mottle by half their range at a species distance near 0.
  - The projection is fitted to non-producers, bodies whose photosynthetic share of volume is under 0.5. The coordinator ordered on 2026-10-29
    that they be pooled over several worlds: the bodies alive at 24,000 s in r62-h6202, r63b's arms A to C on 6202 and 6203,
    and r62cf 6202 to 6204, 2,131 bodies in all. Each parameter is re-centred on them, with its gain and bias folded into the
    weights, so its p10 to p90 spans most of [0, 1]. The output went to `scratch/skin-map/pooled-24000/`, derived data that the tool rebuilds from the runs.
  - Each world's own run of the tool can drop an input that fails that world's parent-and-child check, so its vectors are
    not the pooled ones. Vectors for other worlds are computed from their `features.csv` with the pooled projection and
    weights.
- **The C# port** is on branch `theatre-look-skinmap` (worktree `scratch/wt-skin-map`), behind `EVOSIM_THEATRE_SKIN_MAP`,
  off by default. The commits are `866edae6` (the port), `6f5ec7ee` (the pooled fit and the marine palette),
  `ca8258b5` (producers drawn without the map) and `0ddf3635` (`EVOSIM_STILLS_OWN_ID`, below). `TheatreSkinMapConstants.cs` is generated by
  `scripts/skin-map/gen_constants.py` from the read's `projection.json` and `weights.json`.
  - A non-producer's base hue picks a hue on a path from blue (220 degrees) through violet, magenta and red to gold (50
    degrees), with no greens. The coordinator relayed on 2026-10-29 that the owner wants a glorious variety among animals.
    Every colour of the body turns by the one amount that lands its main tint there, hue alone.
  - A producer gets no vector, so it is drawn exactly as today. The alga look sets its colour per world, by the owner's
    ruling of 2026-10-29.
  - Nine parameters are wired. The carve's taper is not, because it is one material-wide value that the one skin's geometry
    and the knuckles also read.
  - `EVOSIM_THEATRE_SKIN_MAP_LOG=1` logs each body's vector and features, for a parity check against the Python. On the
    third sheet all 19 bodies agreed to 5e-7.
- **The stills** are drawn by `TheatreSoloStill` from lists that `scripts/skin-map/make_stills_list.py` writes, one per run.
  - A solo still with the map off is painted as id 1, and the id seeds each part's carve (`TheatrePalette.Gather`'s
    `SeedOf`). So the map-off row of the third sheet wore a different carve from the map-on row: its producers
    differed in outline and relief, with colour within 3 levels. `EVOSIM_STILLS_OWN_ID=1` paints both runs under the
    row's own id, and step Ѣ sets it. A film paints every body under its true id either way.
  - The C# logs a vector for a producer too, but `TryGet` refuses it, so the sheet shows no skin distance for one.

## Measurements

| Fit | Pairs | Spearman ρ | Corner share | Parent-child, median and p95 | Stranger median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| h6202 alone, all bodies | 16,884 | 0.774 | 0.0% | 0.002, 0.49 | 0.81 |
| pooled non-producers | 16,884, all | 0.758 | 0.0% | 0.002, 0.71 | 1.01 |
| pooled non-producers | 6,012, non-producers | 0.721 | 0.0% | 0.025, 0.71 | 1.26 |

Both of the coordinator's bars (ρ above 0.5, corner share under 5%) pass on every row. Under the h6202 fit, every one of the
74 parent-child pairs with a skin jump over 0.5 at a species distance under 1 came from the body. In about two-thirds the
part count changed. In the rest, one anchor folded a two-part body from end-on to side-on. h6202's animals at 24,000 s are
a few clonal families, so variety among animals shows across worlds rather than within one.

## Sheets

- The first sheet, h6202 alone, is in `scratch/owner/genome-to-skin-family-sheet/` with a README. Kin looked alike, but
  strangers did not look different, because 93% of the bodies it was fitted on were producers.
- The second fit's sheet (`skin-map-sheet-6d.png`, beside this note) showed animals from four worlds in blue, magenta,
  violet, salmon-orange and pink-red, with kin alike. But it turned the producers khaki, which `ca8258b5` fixes.
- The third (`skin-map-sheet-6e.png`, at `ca8258b5`) kept the animals within one level of the
  second, with the producers' colour restored, but its two rows differed in carve, as above.

## The hue, reworked

The fourth sheet showed that the map's hue came almost entirely from diet. On 2026-10-30 the theatre lane screened four
replacements and took a hybrid, `EVOSIM_THEATRE_SKIN_MAP=2`: the base hue is the rank of a lineage score, and diet moves
saturation instead. On screen it spread the animals less than on paper. Three dials followed, each off by default:
`SKIN_MAP_TURN_ALL`, `SKIN_MAP_SAT_FLOOR` and `SKIN_MAP_DEEPEN`. Only deepen shows at sheet size. The lane's pick is the hybrid
with deepen at 0.5. Most eaters still come out pink to magenta, which comes from the palette's path, and that path is the
owner's to rule. The screen, the dials, their numbers and the question put to the owner are in `logbook/specs/skin-map-hue/`.
That folder is on the branch `theatre-look-family`, which merged the skin map at `3bfdef6a` and passed dials-off and both
film-look smokes there.

## Open

- The skin plan's 5(c) still names h6202 alone as the reference population. That file is in main's tree with another
  session's uncommitted edits, so the pooled population goes in by blob when this work is written up.
- `TheatreSkinMap` caches vectors by creature id. A stills list that mixes worlds can reuse one world's vector for another
  world's body with the same id; the sheets so far had no shared ids.
- The pattern's seed and offset come from the pattern key's hash, which changes at a key's bin edge. That is a small flip the
  design meant to avoid (the noise phase was to come from the plan hash).
- The animals' new colours are strongly saturated. The film's water light may soften them, which a film-look render would show.
