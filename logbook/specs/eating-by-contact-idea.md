# An idea, held: eating by contact

*Raised with the owner on 2026-10-23, while round 62 ran. The owner asked for it to be kept as an idea, and ruled
the same morning that nothing else changes until round 62 has shown that the grazers' dials are right. It is not a
proposal.*

## The question

The mouth kills by contact: a part touching a living body damages it each step. It eats by distance. When a
body dies, the world replaces it with one point at its old centre. The point carries the energy and matter, and is
pictured as a sphere at the structural density. It takes no part in the physics: nothing touches it, it sinks at a
fixed speed and it never tumbles. A grazer eats it when the corpse's sphere plus the mouth's reach holds the
grazer's centre. The owner asked why a mouth needs a reach at all if it is a contact tool.

## Why the reach had to be large (inference)

- A grazer bites a leaf at the leaf's edge, but the corpse appears at the leaf's centre, a metre or more away for a
  large leaf. A short reach often cannot eat what the grazer just killed.
- The reach is counted from the grazer's centre, so a body 1 m long needs half a metre more to cover its own length.
- The screens agree with this. At 0.5 m grazers starved, and at 2 m they lived mostly by eating corpses they had not
  made (`logbook/specs/r62-grazer-screen/` on `r62-graze`, `logbook/specs/r62-spoil-screen/`).

## Two ways to make eating a contact

1. **A dead body stays a body until it is eaten**, and a grazer eats where it touches, as it kills. This is the
   closest to an ocean, and the heaviest change: thousands more bodies in the physics, and the corpse rules
   rewritten.
2. **The mouth point with a small reach.** Measure the reach from the mouth (`scratch/r62-box/mouth/definition.md`)
   and set it near the corpse's own size, close to the mouth touching the corpse, at little cost. The gap between
   the bite and the leaf's centre may then need the corpse placed where the body was bitten, not at its centre.

The coordinator's suggestion: once the grazers' dials are confirmed, screen the second, stepping the reach down
toward contact. If grazers cannot pay their way there, ask next where a corpse should appear. Both are world rules,
and the owner's to rule.
