## 2. Most machine-written lines

1. **"On top, a fee of twice the child's body is burnt, never under 50 J."** (Scene 4) — reads like a rule pulled straight from a config file (multiplier + floor value), not something a narrator would say aloud.
2. **"A new rule made a bud the only way a new kind of part can arrive."** (Scene 16) — patch-notes phrasing ("a new rule made X the only way Y can happen") describing a game-mechanic change, not a nature-doc sentence.
3. **"So this round changed four rules in the eaters' favour."** (Scene 7) — a changelog tally ("changed four rules") stated as a bare count with no follow-through, the way a release note lists "4 fixes" without listing them.

## 3. The story, in two sentences

Three parallel simulated tanks re-ran the same 30,000-second experiment to see whether "eater" creatures (stomach, no leaf) could finally establish a lasting lineage after rule changes tilted things their way, and instead every eater line boomed early and then starved out as its members aged and thinned their own food; meanwhile the light-eating "leaf" lines came to dominate all three tanks — in one of them a leaf lineage stumbled into keeping a tiny, barely-used stomach, which is the smaller of the two things the round had hoped for.
