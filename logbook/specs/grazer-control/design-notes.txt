1. HOW DIMENSIONS BECOME PART SIZES
======================================================================================

[code] Developer.Expand: halfExtents = |node.Dimensions x accumulatedScale|, then each axis is
       raised to at least MinPartHalfExtent (0.01). The root's accumulatedScale is
       (AdultScale, AdultScale, AdultScale), and each edge multiplies its own Scale into it.
       So `dimensions` are HALF-extents. Box volume = 8xyz. Box area = 8(xy + yz + zx), built
       from the drag panels (StandardShapes.BoxShape, PartShape.SurfaceArea).
[code] The `growth` gene (Determinate or Indeterminate) is D106's module gene: whether a node's
       count can rise later under the module rule. It is not size growth. Size growth (D087)
       applies to any body born below its adult size, whatever that gene says.
[code] At inoculation (World.Inoculate -> NewbornFrom) the body is planted at
       wanted = investment / brood x (1 - NewbornReserveFraction 0.2).
       If wanted >= 1 the body is planted as the adult, with birthFraction 1.
       Otherwise it is adult.Scaled(wanted^(1/3)), birthFraction = tissue ratio (about wanted),
       and it grows from there.
[computed] With investment 1.3: wanted = 1.3 x 0.8 = 1.04 >= 1, so every file plants an ADULT.
       grazer-04 is planted at 0.40 m a side, grazer-06 at 0.60 m, the plate at 0.74 x 0.06 x 0.74.
       (1.25 would reach exactly 1.0. 1.3 clears the float rounding with margin. At conception the
       share is capped at 1.25 x adult tissue, so the extra 0.05 only raises the breeding gate by
       0.05 x tissue.)
[computed] With 167087's own investment of 0.111401: wanted = 0.0891 and the linear scale is
       0.0891^(1/3) = 0.447. grazer-04 would be planted at 0.179 m a side with a purse of
       200 x 0.0891 = 17.8 J and an area of 0.96 x 0.447^2 = 0.19 m^2. That is 0.096 health a step,
       so about 6 steps to kill a leaf, and the purse goes into growing. Useless as a control.

2. BUOYANCY: WHY THE FILES CARRY NO BLADDER
======================================================================================

[code] Evosim.Dynamics/Fluid.cs (and the same lines in the card kernels, Kernels.F/D.g.cs):
       excess = TissueExcessDensity (0.02) x BuoyancyModel.ExcessDensityFactor(body.TotalVolume,
       NeutralBodyVolume). Then per link: netDensity = excess x (1 - lift_i), and the force is
       -netDensity x mass_i x g / TissueDensity. Mass is volume x one density for every cell type,
       so all cell types sink alike per cubic metre. Summed over the body, the vertical force is
       proportional to sum_i V_i (1 - lift_i). Neutral means sum lift_i V_i = sum V_i, which is the
       brief's rule.
[code] ExcessDensityFactor = max(0, 1 - (V0/V)^(2/3)), and exactly 0 when V <= V0.
[run]  Round 58 config: fluid.neutralBodyVolume = 0.25 m^3.
[computed] So any body whose TOTAL volume is at most 0.25 m^3 feels zero sink, and zero net lift
       whatever its bladders hold. Lift multiplies a zero.
         grazer-04       0.064 m^3   neutral, no bladder needed
         grazer-06       0.216 m^3   neutral, no bladder needed
         plate           0.0329 m^3  neutral
         plate-late      0.0409 m^3  neutral (after the module)
       The median leaf (0.206 m^3) is neutral by the same rule, so its bladders do nothing for
       depth. They only matter through the D111 buoyancy-offset torque.
[computed] The brief's bladder, built anyway: half-extents (0.2, 0.2, 0.1) on the 0.4 m cube,
       lift 3. That gives V_b = 0.032 = V_c/2, and sum lift V = 3 x 0.032 = 0.096 = V_c + V_b,
       exactly neutral. Total 0.096 m^3, still under 0.25, so it does nothing at all. Its cost:
       upkeep 2.5 x 0.032 = 0.080 W, lift 0.05 x 3 x 0.032 = 0.0048 W, support
       0.1 x (0.64/4) x 0.3^2 = 0.0014 W. That is 0.086 W, which shortens the unfed life from
       358 s to 311 s.
[code] It also costs bites. Metabolise.HandOverContacts takes the NEAREST part pair of each
       overlapping body pair, and a bladder has attack 0. A leaf that touches the bladder first
       takes no damage.
       Heavy or light? Neither can be chosen for these bodies: below 0.25 m^3 they are exactly
       neutral. If a body above 0.25 m^3 were ever wanted, I would aim at exactly neutral
       (lift 3, bladder at half the consumer's volume). Being slightly heavy sinks it to the bed
       and slightly light floats it to y = 0, and both leave the leaf layer.
       To add the bladder anyway, append this node and give the root this edge (nextEdgeId 2):
         edge: {"id":"0000000000000001","child":1,"terminalOnly":false,
                "parentAnchor":{"x":1,"y":0,"z":0},"childAnchor":{"x":-1,"y":0,"z":0},
                "scale":{"x":1,"y":1,"z":1},"orientation":{"x":0,"y":0,"z":0,"w":1},
                "reflect":{"x":false,"y":false,"z":false}}
         node: {"cell":"buoyancy","shape":"box","joint":"Fixed","power":0,
                "drive":{"threshold":0,"reaction":0.1,"hold":0.1,"release":0.1},"lift":3,
                "recursiveLimit":1,"growth":"Determinate","maxModules":1,"attack":0,"intake":0,
                "protection":0,"toughness":1,"buoyancyOffset":0,
                "dimensions":{"x":0.1,"y":0.2,"z":0.2},"jointLimits":[],"edges":[],"neurons":[]}
         (The dimensions are half-extents 0.1 along the joining x axis.)

    (founderEndowmentSeconds 600 is a floor and trickle founder's, not an inoculant's) and no D124
    cap. 200 J is the most ANY inoculant can carry in this world: birthFraction <= 1, and the

FINDING A, the important one: for a body WITH A MOUTH, EVOSIM_INOCULATE_DEPTH is ignored in
round 58's world.
[code] Inoculate calls EnsureFounderAcceptance, and in round 58 foundersFollowFood,
       foundersFollowCorpses and foundersFollowFoodDepth are all true [run]. HasMouth(adult)
       means any part with Intake > 0. For such a body:
       - x, z are accepted with p = (corpse joules in the 1 m column) / (richest column's). A
         column with no corpse is never accepted. 512 attempts (AttemptBudget 64 x 8).
       - The depth is replaced by CorpseDepth: a draw in [y_richest_corpse, + intakeReach 0.5 m]
         in that column. It is held at least one body radius under the surface, lifted clear of
         the bed and any reef, and admitted at that height ("atFood").
[run]  At t = 12,000 (stats.jsonl): 10,037 corpses holding 220.5 kJ, of which 9,630 are settled
       (196.5 kJ) and 826 are on reefs (24.6 kJ).
[guessed] So grazer-04, grazer-06 and grazer-plate will be planted right beside corpses: mostly

       not grazing. That tests INTAKE well and ATTACK only if leaves come by.
[code] grazer-plate-late has intake 0 at planting (its mouth is a module not yet built). It has no

4. COSTS, PURSE, BLOW, REACH (round 58 prices)

[code] Standing watts (Metabolism.StepAt at depth, no food): the sum over parts of
       (cell upkeep W/m^3 x V)
       + attackWattsPerUnit x attack x area + intakeWattsPerUnit x intake x area
       + toughness above 1 x 0.1 x V + offset price x |offset| x V
       + support 0.1 x (area/4) x distance-from-root^2,
       all multiplied by the repair factor lifespan^ageRepairExponent = 0.9415158^0.5 = 0.97031.
       Senescence wear is 1 (doubling 0). Consumer upkeep is 6 W/m^3 [run].
[code] Blow (WorldMouth.DamageOf): attack x part area x 0.5 s per metabolic step (0.5 s,
       EnvBinding.MetabolicStepSeconds), minus the defender part's protection x its area. It is
       taken as a share of the pool, volume x toughness x 13. Healing is 0.01/s, paid in J.
       One contact entry per overlapping body pair per step, between the nearest parts.
[code] Intake (WorldMouth.ApplyIntake): sum of intake x area, in units/s of 100 J, from any
       corpse whose centre is within r_corpse + 0.5 m of the body's position.
       r_corpse = (3 x J/500 / 4pi)^(1/3). Shared pro rata among mouths. 20% waste goes to the
       snow. NO handling cost: handlingCostPerJouleEaten applies only to PoolDrawn, the cell
       filter path.

[code] GenomeJson.Read (format 12) requires every one of: format, root, adultScale, lifespan,
       reproduction {brood, investment, margin, mode, gestation}, nodes[], globalBrain[],

[code] Genome.Validate:

       - jointLimits length = joint DOF (Fixed = 0). Power 0 with no joint.

======================================================================================
7. WHAT COULD GO WRONG (check in the smoke)
======================================================================================
- A malformed genome fails only at the assay instant: Read at start-up does not validate. A smoke
  run should resume from a checkpoint and inoculate one step after it.
- D129 placement (finding A): the simple mouths land on corpses. If no column is accepted in 512
  draws, the copy is a crowded stillbirth (unlikely at 220 kJ of corpses).
- plate-late depends on the module rule firing for an inoculant. The code says it will
  (IndeterminateNodes is set in Admit; round 58 has 36 module adds on file), but this has never
  been observed for an inoculant. Read moduleAdds rising by about COUNT within 10 s, and the
  grazers' HasIntake through intakeShare.
- The purse gives a short window (finding B). Read "starved" deaths at about 360 to 490 s after
  planting, unless they ate.
- The bladder and the nearest-part rule are why the files carry no bladder (section 2).
- Leaves with a cuticle above about 0.14 to 0.31 (by body) are immune.
- About 21% age deaths in 2,000 s.
- grazer-06 starves in about 2 minutes. Prefer the plate for a one-step kill.
- The card path: module rebuilds and contacts both occur in round 58's own record, so nothing new
  is asked of the solver. The one-step-kill margin depends on the 0.5 s metabolic step [code].
