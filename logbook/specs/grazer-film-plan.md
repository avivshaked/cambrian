# The grazer film: the plan and its gates

The milestone is one film of a grazer world that runs the new rules. The owner set its aim on 2026-10-28: a cohesive
story that also looks amazing, from a rich world in which grazers survive and evolve. Migration between oases is
welcome, but it is not required (D254). Everything below works towards that film. Work that does not feed it is
parked until the film is made.

Each step ends at a gate with numbers. A step that passes moves on without waiting, and a feature that passes its gate
goes into the round without a further ruling. A step that fails takes the fallback written beside it. A feature that
fails is left out, and the film goes on without it.

## The gates

### G0, for every build

- With every new switch off, a world steps bit for bit with main and at main's pace.
- The filtered suites pass, and the full suite passes before the build lands.
- On the card, with the switch on, the identity sitting gives the same digest at groups 32 and 64.
- With the switch on, the world steps no more than 15 % slower than with it off.

### G2, the bloom screen (D251)

Four arms on two seeds, 24,000 s each: (A) today; (B) the cycles; (C) the cycles and the far smell; (D) as C, with fat.
Fat's crowd re-record and card sitting come first. Each arm is judged in windows of 3,000 s from 6,000 s on.

| Test | Threshold | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| Grazers survive | at least 30 grazers alive in every judged window (D244), and D222's tests pass | gate |
| The bloom reaches the food | each island's lit leaf area follows its influx factor, with a correlation of at least 0.5 at the best lag | gate for B to D |
| Grazers evolve | at least one heritable grazer trait shifts by at least one founder standard deviation between the first and last windows. The traits are body mass, part count, limb push, and the brain's mean drive | gate |
| The world is rich | at least three grazer clades each hold 10 % of grazers in some window, and something eats grazers or their remains | reported |
| Story material | crossings between islands, crowds at blooms, busts and recoveries, all counted | reported |

- **Pass.** Among the arms that pass every gate, the one with the most story material is the film's world. When arms
  tie, the simplest wins. Fat goes into the round if arm D passes every gate.
- **Fallback.** If the leaves do not follow the bloom, the period is lengthened to between 5,000 and 10,000 s, and the
  screen runs again. If no bloom arm passes, the film's world is arm A, today's.

### G7, the combined round

The round runs G2's world with whatever passed G3 and G5. It has three seeds of 48,000 s each, so that each island
blooms several times. It is pre-registered and committed before launch.

| Test | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Grazers survive | at least 30 grazers in every window, in all three seeds |
| Grazers evolve | G2's evolution gate passes in at least two of three seeds |
| A story to tell | the story flow's outline finds at least five beats on one seed, each with footage at film distance. Examples are a bloom and its crowd, a lineage that changes, a predator, a bust and a recovery, a crossing |
| Pace | each world steps at least as fast as real time |

- **Fallback.** If a seed collapses in its second half, the film covers the part of the run that holds.
