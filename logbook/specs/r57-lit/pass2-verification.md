**Sources.** CR = CrossRef (`api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`). OA = OpenAlex (`api.openalex.org/works/doi:<doi>`). EPMC = Europe PMC REST search. S2 = Semantic Scholar Graph API. bRx = bioRxiv details API. PMC = the PubMed Central article page or its XML.

**Retraction.** "None" means the CrossRef record carries no `update-to` or `updated-by` field and its `relation` field names no retraction, correction or expression of concern, and OpenAlex reports `is_retracted: false`. The only relations present are F1000 or Publons review links (Fischer, Starrfelt, Park) and a `has-preprint` link (Weissman).

**Full text.** "Full text" means the whole article was read from a legal open copy. "Abstract only" means no legal open copy could be read; the abstract came from the source named.
