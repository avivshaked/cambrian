**How to read the "Relevance" column.** Each line says where its claim comes from:

- **from the abstract**: the abstract was read in this session (Europe PMC, PMC or arXiv). "(fetcher summary)" means the fetch tool gave a close summary of the abstract rather than its full text.
- **from the abstract (search summary)**: the abstract was not opened. Its content is known only from the search engine's summary of the abstract page. Treat this as weaker.
- **from the title only**: nothing beyond the bibliographic record was seen.

"Why it matters for r57" is my inference about how the paper bears on the round. It is not a claim the paper makes.

Peer review: "yes" means a journal article, an Annual Review or a refereed conference paper. Book chapters are "yes (edited volume)".

## Gaps in this pool

- **Q3 has no model of storage under autocorrelated (red) variation in particular.** Fischer et al. 2011 vary "predictability", but how they define it is not stated in the abstract and needs checking.
- **Q5 is thin.** No artificial-life study was found of evolved storage or bet-hedging under seasonal plus stochastic resource input. That may be a real gap, or a search failure.
- **Seen but not carried:** Simons 2009 (*Lobelia*, Proc. R. Soc. B 276:1987–1992) and McNamara & Houston 1996 (Nature 380:215–221) appeared in results, but no abstract was read. Tuljapurkar et al. 2006 (Ecology Letters) was carried only through search summaries. Any of the three could be added in Pass 2 after reading its abstract.
