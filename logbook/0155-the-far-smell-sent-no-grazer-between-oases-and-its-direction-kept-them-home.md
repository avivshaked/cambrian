# The far smell sent no grazer between oases, and its direction kept them home

*2026-11-01, written by the writing lane the evening the screen ended, from the coordinator's read in
`logbook/specs/r65-read/README.md` and the pre-registration in `logbook/specs/r65-prereg/predictions.md`, committed
before any world ran. Every figure is measured unless it says otherwise.*

The smell screen r65 asked whether a leaf smell that the water carries would send grazers from one oasis to the next.
It did not. On either seed, neither form of the smell raised the leaves' share of a grazer's food or sent grazers
travelling. Neither brought them sooner to a blooming island, and the pace held. The form that also told each grazer
which way the smell grew kept its grazers closer to home. Grazer numbers rose with the smell on one seed and fell on
the other, so the seed set them. For the question of memory, the screen says something narrow. A grazer handed the
direction that a memory would have to work out still did not travel.

## The question

Today a grazer between groves smells nothing. The leaves' smell ends at 6 m, and groves lie 20 to 30 m apart. D270
added a far smell, held on a 5 m grid and carried by the current. It also added a current sense that tells each part
which way the water runs over the ground. The screen ran it in two forms (D262). The realistic form, R, reads only how
strong the far smell is. The overpowered form, O, also reads which way it grows. Each ran against C, the same world
with no far smell.

The coordinator pre-registered the screen before any of it ran. Six worlds ran 24,000 s each on the card: C, R and O
on seeds 6202 and 6203. They ran in round 64's arm D under true-shape contact, so the answer would apply to the film's
round (D272). Every world ended on its budget, and the energy accounts closed in all six.

A crossing is a grazer's move from one grove to another, counted with a gap of 10 m. The gather lag is how long a
blooming island's grazer crowd trails its bloom. Every world held the floor of 30 grazers in every judged window from
12,000 s.

That is the outcome the pre-registration called "both fail". By its own rule, the nursery comes before another world
screen.

## What held on both seeds

O kept grazers nearer the groves. The share of grazer position fixes more than 20 m past every grove was 0.74 and 0.62
of C's under O. O's newborns took 38 to 44% longer to their first bite on both seeds. On 6203 O's crossings fell to
0.68 of C's. The read's inference is that inside a grove the direction points every grazer at that grove's own centre.
So it holds them at home instead of sending them to the next. The pre-registration had named this as a way O could
fail.

R travelled no more than C. Its crossings were 0.33 and 0.71 of C's, below C on both seeds.

## What did not hold

Grazer numbers did not follow the smell. On 6202, R and O held 75% and 33% more grazers than C in the last window. On
6203 they held 47% and 55% fewer. Grazer births over the run swung the same way. The two seeds disagree in sign, so
something other than the smell set the size of a grazer population here.

While the screen ran, the coordinator wrote an interim note on 6202. It read R's larger crowd as grazers perhaps
gathering in the carried smell, slowing where it was strong. That note did not survive the second seed, where R's
crowd was half of C's.

The leaves split the same way. O's leaves ended at 1.18 of C's on 6202 and 0.59 on 6203, and R's at 1.42 and 0.79.

## What follows

The screen decides nothing for the film's round. D272 keeps the smell off there, and turning it on is the owner's
ruling. By the pre-registration, the nursery comes next.

For memory, the question of logbook 0154, the answer is narrower than hoped. O was handed the direction that a memory
would have to work out, and its grazers still did not travel between oases in 24,000 s. They stayed closer to home. So
within a run of this length, what the grazers lacked was not information about direction. The read's inference is that
a brain does not gain from a sense it has not yet learned to use. The smell's design had warned that founder wiring
cannot cast, the side-to-side search a tracker makes when it loses a smell. A memory unit would add a capability that
a world screen of this length cannot show being used (the read's inference).

The test that can show it is a nursery task. It would reward climbing a smell that gives strength only, run with
brains that have a time constant per unit and brains that do not. The coordinator puts that test, with the time
constant as a world rule entering overpowered (D262), to the owner as one proposal.

I find the result clarifying rather than disappointing. The smell was the obvious missing piece, and handing it over
whole, direction included, changed where grazers stayed and not whether they left.
