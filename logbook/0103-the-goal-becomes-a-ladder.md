# 0103 — The goal becomes a ladder

**2026-09-16, morning**  ·  the owner retired the campaign's pass-or-fail bar on reading round 38, and replaced it with a ladder of milestones; the ruling is [D094](../DECISIONS.md#d094), the deprecated rules stay in the record marked

---

## What happened

The morning update after round 38's read (0101) said the plain thing: the world holds at
1,600 bodies in every seed, the eaters boom to a third of the living and starve, a fresh
line rises after them, and the goal rule fails five of five. The owner's reply was that a
goal proven once does not need proving again, that selection is visibly working, that the
future is more cell types and senses, and that the original goal no longer serves. Then:
think it through fully, in the open, and recommend something I can stand behind.

## The train of thought, kept

The reasoning is in D094; the shape of it is worth keeping here as it was made, because
the first answer was not the last one.

First I ranked what the project needs from a goal. Honesty under pressure came first,
because the pre-registration discipline is the most valuable thing the campaign owns and
every reviewer has said so. Evidence of adaptation, not only selection, came second: the
campaign has shown lines rising and falling on their economics many times and a trait
moving by degree because it pays exactly once (0082). A world rich enough to have knobs
came third, and mesmerising, the design's first aim, fourth, because it is served by the
second and third and cannot be scored. A single pass-or-fail number, the reviewers'
balance clauses and the continuity of old verdicts ranked below those.

Then four candidates. Loosen D063 so a cycling world passes: a passed test, and every
loosening invites the next. Predictions only, no long horizon: honest, and already nearly
the practice since D091, but a hundred rounds later nobody could say whether the project
was closer to anything. A new pass-or-fail bar for adaptation, set now: it points at the
real gap, but the instrument does not exist, and a bar set before its instrument is set
wrong, as 0102's E6 was the night before. A ladder of milestones, each a bar until met
and a reading after: it fits how the project has moved, gives the horizon, and its danger
(tuning the world until a rung passes) is the one the pre-registration already guards.

My first instinct, said in the morning update, had been the second candidate with two
standing readings. Working through the ranking changed it: the ladder with the adaptation
reading inside it, the per-round predictions as the rule of every round, and D063 demoted
to a regression check. One more revision on the way: I had meant to leave the scorer's
verdict line alone, and realised that leaves PASS and FAIL in every table for a rung that
is closed, and people read those words. So the scorer prints a state.

## The ruling

The owner asked for it in simple words, then asked the question that mattered: with
pass-or-fail gone, can we still tell whether a round succeeded, and by how much? Yes,
twice over: every round is still judged prediction by prediction against thresholds
committed before launch, and the ladder says which rung the campaign stands on. What goes
is only the habit of stamping every round with a bar that had stopped measuring progress.
The owner ruled it, with one instruction on the record: the old rules are deprecated and
marked, never overwritten. D063 and D081 stand as written with a note under each title.

## What it changes in practice

- The scorer's verdict line becomes a state per seed: *the chain holds*, *the chain
  cycles* (with the period), or *no chain*; every number stays; PASS and FAIL go. Queued
  with its 52 fixture assertions.
- A trait-by-degree instrument, 0082's reading made on every round from the snapshots, is
  built with the timeline so rung 2 can be measured on three rounds before its threshold
  is set.
- Round 39's pre-registration (0102) is unchanged: it never required the goal rule, and
  its eleven predictions are what the round is read on.
- HANDOFF's goal paragraph and table are kept as the record of the bar through round 38,
  with the ladder above them.

One thing of my own to add. I had written the morning's first recommendation in a few
minutes and would have defended it. Being asked to show the train of thought is what found the two
places it was wrong. The record should keep asking for that.
