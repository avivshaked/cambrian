# 0103 — The goal becomes a ladder

**2026-09-16, morning**  ·  the owner retired the campaign's pass-or-fail bar on reading round 38 and replaced it with a ladder of milestones. The ruling is [D094](../DECISIONS.md#d094); the retired rules stay where they are, marked as deprecated

## What happened

The morning update after round 38's read (0101) said the plain thing. The world holds at
1,600 bodies in every seed. The eaters boom to a third of the living and starve, a fresh
line rises after them, and the goal rule fails five of five.

The owner's reply was that a goal proven once does not need proving again. The reply went
on: selection is visibly working, the future is more cell types and senses, and the
original goal no longer serves. Then: think it through fully, in the open, and recommend
something I can stand behind.

## The train of thought, kept

The reasoning is in D094. The shape of it belongs here as it was made, because the first
answer was not the last one.

First I ranked what the project needs from a goal. Honesty under pressure came first,
because the pre-registration discipline is the most valuable thing the campaign owns and
every reviewer has said so. Evidence of adaptation, not only selection, came second. The
campaign has shown lines rising and falling on their economics many times, while a trait
that moved by degree because it paid has come up once, in 0082. A world rich enough to
have knobs came third. Mesmerising, the design's first aim, came fourth, because the
second and third serve it and it cannot be scored. A single pass-or-fail number, the
reviewers' balance clauses and the continuity of old verdicts ranked below those.

Then I weighed four candidates. The first was to loosen D063 so a cycling world passes,
which is a passed test, and every loosening invites the next. The second was predictions
only, with no long horizon. That claims nothing it cannot show, and is already nearly the
practice since D091, but nobody could say a hundred rounds later whether the project was
closer to anything. The third was a new pass-or-fail bar for adaptation, set now. It points
at the real gap, but the instrument does not exist, and a bar set before its instrument is
set wrong, as 0102's E6 was the night before. The fourth was a ladder of milestones, each a
bar until met and a reading after. It fits how the project has moved and gives the horizon,
and its danger, tuning the world until a rung passes, is the one the pre-registration
already guards.

My first instinct, said in the morning update, had been the second candidate with two
standing readings. Working through the ranking changed it. What came out was the ladder
with the adaptation reading inside it, the per-round predictions as the rule of every
round, and D063 demoted to a regression check.

There was one more revision on the way. I had meant to leave the scorer's verdict line
alone, and then realised that leaves PASS and FAIL in every table for a rung that is
closed. People read those words, so the scorer prints a state.

## The ruling

The owner asked for it in simple words. Then came the question that mattered: with
pass-or-fail gone, could we still tell whether a round succeeded, and by how much. The
answer is yes, twice over. Every round is still judged prediction by prediction against
thresholds committed before launch, and the ladder says which rung the campaign stands on.
What goes is only the habit of stamping every round with a bar that had stopped measuring
progress. The owner ruled it, and added one instruction: the old rules are deprecated and
marked, never overwritten. D063 and D081 stand as written with a note under each title.

## What it changes in practice

- The scorer's verdict line becomes a state per seed: *the chain holds*, *the chain
  cycles* with the period, or *no chain*. Every number stays, and PASS and FAIL go. It is
  queued with its 52 fixture assertions.
- A trait-by-degree instrument is built with the timeline: 0082's reading, made on every
  round from the snapshots. Rung 2 can then be measured on three rounds before its
  threshold is set.
- Round 39's pre-registration (0102) is unchanged: it never required the goal rule, and
  its eleven predictions are what the round is read on.
- HANDOFF's goal paragraph and table stay as written, since they say what the bar was
  through round 38, and the ladder goes above them.

One thing of my own to add. I had written the morning's first recommendation in a few
minutes and would have defended it. Being asked to show the train of thought is what found
the two places it was wrong. We should keep asking for that.
