## What the second draw says

The mixed disc is the world's. Fifteen rim readings of fifteen sit between 17 and 24% on a
quarter-area ring. The drift is a fifth of a centimetre per hundred seconds against the
fully-mixed bound. Every column of the footprint is occupied from 5,000 s to the end. Ten
seeds of ten now read the same on the tank with the force, and the centrifuge of round 37
(D090) is gone in every draw.

The eaters are the world's. Ten seeds of ten pass D063 as amended on this base. In the
fresh five every passing clade is a rigid eater, four founded after 1,000 s and one at
470 s, none a founder's own line. The inherited eater count holds at ten or more to the
last sample in every seed. D081's reference bar for the base is ten of ten from here. The
eater populations run larger in the second draw: seed 8 peaked at 972 absorptive bodies,
the largest of either draw. The cycle is tighter too, peak to trough 1.12 to 1.47 after
5,000 s against 1.79 to 2.35 in 37b.

The joint survives founding in three draws of ten and grows in none. One fresh seed holds
a standing jointed population at the end, against two in 37b, so three of ten. In every
fresh seed the jointed count peaks before 3,000 s (174, 110, 77, 121, 29) and decays from
there. Three seeds reach zero near 11,000 s and stay there. Seed 6's 14 at the end are the
tail of a peak at 174 rather than a line that grew, and round 37b's two standing
populations had the same shape. So the reading of rounds 37 and 37b is neither of 0098's
two-sided cases. The joint is not the lotteries' alone, since it stands in a second draw.
It is not the world's to keep either, since it never recruits. Round 41's stroke price, and
the fluid terms before it, are read against a joint that survives founding a third of the
time and is then lost.

The tank with the force threw nothing in five new seeds: zero divergences over 3.79 million
jointed body-seconds. At 37b's pooled rate of 3.65 per million that exposure would predict
about fourteen. But 37b's 43 throws were not pooled in kind. Thirty-seven were one hinge
lineage in seed 5, and seeds 1 and 2 threw nothing over 4 million body-seconds of their own
(0097's addendum). Over the ten seeds the rate is 43 over 15.6 million, 2.8 per million,
with every throw in three seeds and most in one lineage. As inference, the throw is a body
plan's and not the world's. The second draw is consistent with that reading without
testing it. The trace's second pass, which keeps the last finite frames, is still the
instrument that will say what the step before the blow-up was. The fresh seeds also carried
a third of 37b's jointed exposure, and the seed with most of it, seed 6 at 2.1 million,
threw nothing.

Stillbirths are open, and the lineage reading does not hold on them. The five read 439, 0,
130, 104 and 709 at the end (37b: 12 to 449). The crowding count is 0 to 31 for the whole
run, so crowding is again excluded. 0097's addendum read stillbirths as riding with the
jointed lineages. Seed 7 fits, with no stillbirths and the joint gone earliest. Seed 10
does not: 709 stillbirths on the smallest jointed exposure of the ten. A stillbirth is a
genome that develops into no parts, a development count and not a physics one, and nothing
in a run's output separates its causes. The reading stays open until an instrument names
the genome that failed.

There are no pockets by ring, as before. The largest ring's share of the detritus is 0.28
against 0.25 for even, and the ring deviation 0.04 to 0.05. A ring statistic cannot see a
smaller pocket; the field's coefficient of variation reads round 38.

readings are the world's. The base's reference count is ten seeds of ten on the goal rule
and a mixed disc in ten of ten. Its joint survives founding in three of ten and grows in
none, and its 43 throws sit in three seeds of ten, lineage-bound. Nothing is adopted or
rejected on a batch. Round 38, the dilute tank, is next. It runs on the build that carries
