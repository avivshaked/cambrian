# 0084 — What still stands

**2026-09-10**  ·  the record triaged against the ribbons (0083); a draft, to be read again with the fixed world on the screen

The owner's question, after 0083: which of the experiments holds any merit, and which should
be retried in a real three-dimensional world. This is the answer as it can be given before
that world has run, in four bins, with the reason beside each item. It was drafted from a
reading of every decision from D040 and every entry from 0030 by an agent, and re-read by me.
Where it says a result should be retried, it says what the retry is.

## The confound is not uniform

The rounds fall into three regimes, and the ribbon bit each differently.

- **Rounds 1 to 24, the tiled world.** A body fed from its whole layer of its patch and x and
  z were, in the record's own words, cosmetic. Nothing horizontal was modelled, so nothing
  horizontal was measured. These rounds are not confounded by columns; they were never
  spatial at all.
- **Rounds 25 to 29, the shared box on the cell fields.** Bodies had positions and could
  touch, but the field still held one number per layer and patch, so a column of three
  hundred bodies fed from a 10 by 10 by 1 m slab. The ribbon existed and did not yet drain
  its own food.
- **Rounds 30 to 33, the vertex water and the grid.** A mouth reads local water. From here a
  column drains its own metre, and since D086 its own cell. This is where the confound bites
  hardest, and it is exactly where the goal rule fell from five of five to two.

One more fact, and it is not fixed by dispersal. The light field charges self-shading as a
mean over the whole patch: a metre-wide ribbon inside a hundred square metres is shaded as
if spread across all of it. The light economy has been generous to every column, and a
dispersal disc still sits inside one patch. If neighbours are meant to compete for light,
shading needs a length scale smaller than a patch, and that is a ruling of its own.

## 1. Holds as measured, and position cannot have entered

- **The energy audit and the matter identity** (D029, 0008; §0p, 0078). A conservation sum
  has no x. The 0.0000% residual and the matter identity that caught a 22,000-unit
  fabrication are true of any geometry, and they are why the rest can be trusted.
- **The prices** (D026, D044, D046, D065): tissue, matter, density, green upkeep. Arithmetic
  identities enforced by tests, not measurements of a world.
- **The ledger calculator and the invasion assay's arithmetic** (D069, 0051). One body's watts,
  excluding shading, depletion and drift by its own statement.
- **Senescence** (D038, 0039) and the recruitment-collapse mechanism (0043). Per-body age laws
  and demographic thresholds.
- **The goal rule's definition and the clade scorer** (D063). A parent-chain walk with four
  clauses. The verdicts are in the next bin.
- **The step ladder, the limiters and the divergence guards** (0052, 0059). Numerical facts
  about the integrator.
- **The crowd costs nothing** (0064). Measured in the most packed arrangement the world can
  make, so an upper bound; dispersal can only lower it.
- **The build invariants**: the config guards, refuse-rather-than-default, the RNG, the
  writer's one-row-one-line rule, the hashes (D034, D078).
- **The aggregate light cap** (D023, §5A.2b): total income is at most irradiance times area.
  The competition it was meant to produce is in the next bin.
- **Replay identity on one thread** (D078, 0069, 0070), as of round 28. The theatre's replay
  parted from round 33 at 200 s for a reason found the same day (0083's addendum), and until
  that fix is validated this holds as of round 28 and not as of today.

## 2. Holds, and was read in a narrower world than anyone believed

- **The goal-rule verdicts** of rounds 18, 28, 30 to 33. Every pass is real, and every pass
  means that a metre-wide column of a clade held a breeding stomach line. "The world holds a
  food chain" has never been shown; "a column does" has, repeatedly.
- **The dials' walk** (D087, 0082). Five seeds agreeing is strong evidence of a push. Every
  body in those seeds competed only with kin at arm's length, and a large litter of small
  bodies is what a packed clade with no dispersal selects for. The neutral control is still
  the test, and now so is the dispersed world.
- **Carrying capacity from density** (§5A.2b). The cap holds; "fully contested light" was a
  mean over a patch that the ribbon violated by two orders of magnitude in local density.
- **A stomach line evolves at the vent from nothing, repeatedly** (D077, 0065, 0067). True,
  and it is a claim about one spot, the spot the columns stood on.
- **Fecundity is set by matter, not by energy** (D072, D073, 0056, 0057). The arithmetic
  holds; the contest it describes was among one column's kin in one cell.
- **The floor as founder contamination** (D021, D025, 0029), with an edge nobody read: floor
  spawns are placed at random across the box, so a floor-fed world was more dispersed than a
  self-sustaining one.
- **The depth readings, the surface film, the top and the bed** (0061, 0065, 0067). Vertical
  structure was always modelled. Only the horizontal was invisible.

## 3. Confounded by the columns, and retried in the three-dimensional world

- **The grid against the vertex water** (D086, 0079). Four of five to two, every stomach line
  thinner from founding, and the columns could not say why. D086's own experiment gives the
  mechanism: five mouths in one cell take 1 J between them where five spread take 5 J. Retry:
  round 32's launcher, five seeds at 0.01, dispersed against undispersed on the same seeds,
  read on `cols`, `cols abs` and per-guild recruitment.
- **The prize for moving** (D083, D085, 0074 to 0077; and 0027 onward, D082). Every measured
  prize came from a lone mouth in open water; every run had a lineage stacked in a column.
  The cost side stands. The prize side is unmeasured. Retry: the sitter and mover experiments
  at column density against dispersed, then the free-joint arms (0080) on the dispersed world
  with a per-guild feeding trace.
- **The price was not what kept a sense** (D082, 0075). Drawn in a world with no gradient to
  follow. Re-read after the dispersed round; the prices themselves need no change yet.
- **The mixing rulings** (D084, D085). Both chosen to control how long a lone body's hole
  persists. Retry: a two-by-two screen, mixing at 0.2 and 0.02 against dispersal at 0 and on,
  fast step, two seeds.
- **The exudate-pair reading** (0077). The pair is the column: a stomach born touching its
  producer parent for the whole run. The single reading most directly manufactured by the
  placer. Retry: round 31's arms dispersed, before the exudate lever is pulled.
- **Matter at depth, the deep larder, the stock's slope, `mat blk`** (D071, D074, 0055, 0058,
  0060, 0061, 0068). Every one is a claim about where matter is relative to where bodies are,
  and on the grid a column's conceptions all draw from one 5 m cell. Retry: rounds 32 and 33
  dispersed, `mat blk` against `mat here` per cell, before any dose is touched.
- **The crowd** (D077, 0065, 0068): `crowded` at two to thirty-seven times births, most bodies
  in two patches. Largely the placer's own random walk piling a clade onto one spot. Retry:
  the same arms dispersed; what remains of `crowded` is the vent's real crowding.
- **Corpses as particles** (D086 clause 5). A corpse drifting downstream matters only if
  somebody is downstream; in a column its stock fell back on its own clade. Retry: 0079's
  corpses-off control, dispersed.
- **The returning current, the standing waves and the vent's plume** (D059, D037, D067).
  Their premise, "nothing reads horizontal position", has been false since D083. Their
  results are void as transport claims, and the current is being rebuilt.
- **The patchy world and the roll cells** (D061, D066, 0044, 0048). Horizontal structure
  tested where horizontal position did not exist. Their failures say nothing about a world
  where it does.

## 4. Retired or moot

- **A newborn beside its parent** (D077 clause 5). Retired by the dispersal build; it needs
  its own superseding entry, since it is the root cause and has none.
- **The vertex water as the base** (D083), already superseded by D086.
- **Remineralisation** (D051), measured redundant at any mixing above zero and moot again at
  0.02.
- **The seabed refuge and the floor pantry** (D055, 0042, 0043). Rejected twice; their premise,
  whole-layer access to an unguarded hoard, does not exist on a grid.
- **The matter sink and the conception orders** (D071, D072, D073): screened, not adopted;
  D073's core reading, that energy buys survival and nothing else, is worth re-asking once
  matter is drawn from more than a handful of cells.

## What the owner has to rule again

The decisions that need a superseding or re-read entry once the fixed world has run: D077
(clause 5, retired), D059 and D037 (the current's premise), D086 and D083 (their prize
arithmetic and round 32's verdict), D084 and D085 (mixing), D082 (the prize side), D087
(the walk, and the placer's birth-size reservation), D063's verdicts, D070 and D085's
exudate clause, D074's doses; D061 and D066 marked moot.

The knobs, each a world rule:

1. **Dispersal**: the radius (5 m is a quarter of the length and the whole of the width);
   horizontal at the parent's depth or in three dimensions; a world constant or a dial the
   genome can carry.
2. **The box's shape.** Five metres of width cannot hold a dispersal disc. Length, width and
   patch count are ecological now, and the area is still the sun's aperture, so changing them
   changes the light budget.
3. **The current**: shape, speed, whether bodies ride it, the vent's plume.
4. **Mixing, all four rates and both cell sizes**, chosen against a lone sitter's hole.
5. **D082's four prices**, set against a prize that did not exist; round 34 runs before any
   is reset.
6. **Corpse decay, influx and burial.**
7. **The newborn mass floor**, which the dials walked into in a column.
8. **Shading's length scale**, which dispersal does not touch.
