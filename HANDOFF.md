A screen passes on D222's bar, which is D215's three tests with test 3 changed to say living food. Three screens have run on it. **Why grazers starve: search, not the meal.** Two design reads and a budget agree. A grazer's mouth bites about 0.5-1% of the time, and each bite already takes a whole leaf part (`logbook/specs/kill-rule-read.md`). A meal takes about 100 s, mostly travel, against about 41 s allowed. Eating is about 2,000 times faster than it needs to be. Bred grazers die 15-20 s after birth, starved, because a newborn's reserve covers less than a third of one search. The cost dials cannot close the gap (all four together give about 1.3 times). The owner ruled on 2026-10-25 that a mouth must touch to graze, and that this does not change. The universal fix the owner asked for is `logbook/specs/pace-of-life-read.md`: a pace factor k (physics seconds per world second, the economy untouched, about 3 times the wall time at k = 3), with the newborn reserve raised from 0.2 to about 0.5.
