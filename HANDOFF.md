**Why grazers starve: search, not the meal.** Two design reads and a budget agree. A grazer's mouth bites about 0.5-1% of the time, and each bite already takes a whole leaf part (`logbook/specs/kill-rule-read.md`). A meal takes about 100 s, mostly travel, against about 41 s allowed. Eating is about 2,000 times faster than it needs to be. Bred grazers die 15-20 s after birth, starved, because a newborn's reserve covers less than a third of one search. The cost dials cannot close the gap (all four together give about 1.3 times). The owner ruled on 2026-10-25 that a mouth must touch to graze, and that this does not change. The universal fix the owner asked for is `logbook/specs/pace-of-life-read.md`: a pace factor k (physics seconds per world second, the economy untouched, about 3 times the wall time at k = 3), with the newborn reserve raised from 0.2 to about 0.5. ## The machine

- **Heat and headroom are the bar, not load** (the owner, 2026-10-09). A card group alone peaks at about 79-88 °C. With a Unity process or a build beside it, it has reached 90-95 °C. - **Renders and helpers.** A render may run beside a card world (the owner, 2026-10-26). It runs as one Unity at a time on 16-19 (mask `0x0F0000`) at BelowNormal, under b0's 1 s guard.
