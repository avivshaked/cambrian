# The four long worlds: the plan (2026-11-05)

*Prepared by the coordinator for the owner. It follows the route the owner set on the evening of 2026-11-04. That
route is to finish the nursery and choose its best founders. Then four worlds run long with the shape family on, and
further if they get interesting, and the film is built on that. The facts behind this plan are in
`scratch/four-worlds/facts.md`. One ruling is asked, at the end.*

## Each world is round 66's arm M with three changes

Round 66's world is a 300 m square of open ocean with its edges joined. It holds eleven islands whose plants bloom and
fade, and a current of 0.02 m/s. Its contact is between bodies' true shapes (D272), and a bite needs the mouth on the
food (D275). Eating is by contact, and a killer swallows what is left of its kill (the kill gut).

The changes:

1. **The shape family is on**, as round 63's shape screen ran it (G5, which passed). That is three settings. The
   tapered superellipsoid joins box, sphere and capsule as a part's shape. A limb strokes by the family's fin law. The
   form genes mutate at 0.08. G5's world differed from its control in these three settings and no others.
2. **The forager kit is on**, because the founders were evolved with it and lean on it. It has four parts:
   - The mouth feels how near it is to another body (D280), out to 8 mouth radii, and a bite reaches 3.
   - Where the mouth sits on the body is inherited (D283).
   - The mouth senses the bearing of the nearest leaf and the nearest animal within 4 m (D292).
   - Every brain unit inherits a time constant, which lets a brain hold a reading for seconds (D293).

   In the nursery the best founders ate 350 J. Knocking out the bearing cost them 64.5 J of it, and knocking out the
   feel 79.6 J. The log-scale hunger sense (D294) and the breeding urge (D299) stay off. The founders never had them,
   and neither has been tried in a world.
3. **Four seeds share one set of islands.** Seeds 6201 to 6204 use round 66's islands, whose layout has its own seed.
   So the four worlds differ only in the life that grows in them.

## The founders come from the nursery in two steps

The 40-generation run, the bearing at 4 m (D303), ended its search at 04:33 today. Its exam runs until about 10:00. The
exam's 45 genomes are already written as a pool. Every one of them shares one body, a box with a capsule tail, because
the kit's runs evolved brains alone.

Then one more nursery arm, the shaped arm, evolves body and brain together from those 45, with the family on. This is
where the bodies diverge. Its recipe is drafted and passes the build's check, and a short smoke test must still show
parts mutating into the new shape. It runs a fixed 40 generations. It runs without crossover, which the nursery refuses
when the whole genome mutates. At the 40-generation run's pace its search takes about six hours. Its exam keeps four of
the seven brain conditions: own, brainless, and the bearing and the feel knocked out. That brings the exam to about
three hours (inference).

The shaped arm's exam pool becomes each world's founding pool, every genome at equal weight. Until the floor closes at
3,000 s, half the floor's founders are copies from the pool. The other half are random genomes, which give the world
its plants. After that, founders trickle in at one every 30 s, half of them from the pool, as in round 66.

The owner asked for many founders across many lineages. In numbers that is about 45 genomes. They descend from fewer
than ten root lineages, the direction tops that the mouth retrain started from (inference from the recipes' founders).

## The card runs them, once it carries the kit

The card runs the four worlds as one group. The CPU farm would likely fall under half real time with four worlds at
once (inference from recorded paces).

The card cannot run them yet, because it refuses the feel, the bearing and the time constants. Its copies of all three
are written (branch `card-kit`) and not yet compiled. After the compile they need their tests. Then a card sitting of
about 72 minutes proves each switch identical to the host, bit for bit.

The card can carry the bearing bit for bit only if the bearing is refreshed once a metabolic step, every 50 physics
steps or 0.5 s. The founders evolved with it refreshed every step. So the 40-generation tops are examined again with
the bearing held for 50 steps, which takes about an hour. If the cost is small, the shaped arm evolves at that hold from
its start. The brains that found the worlds then evolve with the bearing as the card will carry it. If the bearing
loses most of its value at that hold, that is a new fork, and it comes to the owner. The hold is the kit's own dial,
delegated under D291, so I set it.

Round 66 ran three worlds in a group at 2.1 to 2.6 times real time. Four worlds with the family on will be slower,
perhaps about 1.5 times (inference). A short founded bench of the group measures it before the launch.

## They run open-ended and are looked at from 12,000 s

The worlds run open-ended, with a checkpoint every 1,500 s. A world stops only if it is empty with nothing coming back,
or if it runs under half real time (the owner, 2026-11-04 and 2026-10-29).

The first look comes at about 12,000 s, where round 63's grazers crashed, and a look follows every 6,000 s after. At
each look `scripts/what-evolved.py` reports who lives and what has changed against the founders. Pictures are filmed
from the nearest checkpoint, as c8 filmed round 66. A crash is a story, and evolved creatures matter most (the owner,
2026-11-04). Each look's findings and pictures go to the owner.

## The ruling asked

The worlds put the kit's switches, and the family's, into a world for the first time. So the rule set goes to the owner
before the launch. It is round 66's arm M, plus the shape family and the forager kit, with hunger and the breeding urge
off. I recommend it, because the founders evolved under these rules and lose 80 of their 350 J without the feel. The
risk is that the kit has never run in a world, only in the nursery's tank. The four seeds are the kit's world screen as
well as the film's material.

Everything before the launch is agent work and goes ahead meanwhile. Nothing waits on the owner until about 23:00.
